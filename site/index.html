Assumptions

  • Boundary: external regulated 5 V and USB, through Q1/D1, to V5_SYS. The external source stands for either the 2S–6S buck or the 1S boost at its 5 V output; both live off-board and are not simulated. Raw battery voltage and charging are not in the model.
  • Load: one explicit demand of 0.5 A at V5_SYS (an assumption, not a measured budget); extra external 5 V demand 0 A. No regulator or LDO control loops are modelled.
  • Sources: external 5 V through an assumed 0.05 Ω; USB 5 V through an assumed 0.35 Ω. No source current limit.
  • Timing: loads start at 2 ms; source events at 8 ms; event window 8–12 ms; “final” is the 19–20 ms average.
  • Models: vendor AOS model for Q1, hand-fitted forward curve for D1 (temperature-dependent leakage not bounded), approximate white LED, nominal capacitors.
  • Out of scope: source current limiting, charging, cell cutoff, USB negotiation, regulator outputs, noise, layout parasitics and thermal behaviour. This simulation cannot decide whether the sensor supplies need an LDO.
