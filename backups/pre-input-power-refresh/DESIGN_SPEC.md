# MARV V2 design specification

## Purpose and scope

Flight controller for rockets and drones, built to **stack on a MicoAir AM32 4-in-1 ESC** (2-6S, no BEC of its own). **Regulated 5 V comes in from an external BEC** on the ESC's FC-connection pad row (J3 pad 7, `5V_IN`); USB is the second source through a P-FET/Schottky OR. Raw pack reaches the board only as a sense wire (J3 pad 9). MCU **RP2354B** (QFN-80, 2 MB in-package flash). Onboard sensors: ICM-45686 (6-axis IMU), BMP581, ADXL375. Logging: a latched (push-push) microSD socket, plus an **optional, unpopulated** QSPI expansion socket on the back that takes either a W25Q32 NOR flash or an APS6404L PSRAM. Firmware and logs use the RP2354B's in-package 2 MB flash and the microSD; the socket is a user expansion, not a board feature. From the ESC the board also reads analog current sense and one-wire KISS telemetry. External daughterboards on solder-pad ports: GPS (UART), ELRS radio (UART), magnetometer (I2C) — they carry their own circuitry. Design rule: the simplest form of what the user states; for unspecified details, what flight controllers in the wild do.

The schematic implements power, ports, MCU, sensors, logging and PWM outputs: 12 sheets, 106 components, 95 nets, generated by `tools/build_power.py` (idempotent — it overwrites generated sheets, so edit the generator, not the sheets). Firmware and PCB layout are not done. Not for manufacture or flight.

## Power inputs

| Interface | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| J3 ESC pad row | **1x9** solder pads. Pads 1-6 are the order printed on the MicoAir AM32 ESC, read left to right: **CURR, TX, M4, M3, M2, M1**; pads 7-9 are this board's power entry: **5V, GND, VBAT**. CURR is the ESC's analog current-sense output (12.75 mV/A, 0-3.3 V). TX is the ESC's 3.3 V one-wire KISS telemetry output, 115200 baud. M1-M4 are 3.3 V DShot/PWM inputs. Pad 7 is `5V_IN`, regulated 5 V from an **external BEC**; pad 9 is `VBAT`, the raw pack (6-25.2 V, 2-6S) as a **sense wire only** — it feeds the R27/R28 divider and nothing else, carries no load and gets no copper zone. |
| External BEC | **Off-board and not optional**: the AM32 ESC has none, so a 2-6S BEC module (or a 1S boost) is wired from the pack to J3 pad 7. Its output capacitance, inrush and transient behaviour are outside this board. What the board provides on 5V_IN is C19 100 uF polymer at the pad row (the C20/C21 local bulk that used to sit at the IO block is removed). **No TVS and no reverse protection** on pads 7 or 9 by design. |
| J4 USB-C | USB 2.0 data and 5 V bench power; no PD; no on-board current limiter (standard FC practice); startup budget is a firmware matter. |
| V5_SYS | **P-FET + Schottky OR**: Q1 AO3401A (drain `5V_IN`, source `V5_SYS`, gate `USB_VBUS` with R7 100k to GND) and D1 1N5819WS (anode `USB_VBUS`, cathode `V5_SYS`). USB present turns Q1 off, so a host can never back-feed the BEC, the servo rows or the ESC. See Decisions and the OR paragraph below. |

FB (pin 1) is a **sense** input on the fixed-output parts and goes straight to V3V3_SYS — Sec 9 "Setting the Output Voltage": only the adjustable AP63200/AP63201 take a divider (Eq.6). So there is **no feedback divider, no 0.1 % resistor pair and no setpoint arithmetic** on this board any more: VOUT is the part number. EN (pin 2) is tied to VIN through **R52 100k**; the datasheet allows a direct connection ("The EN pin is a high voltage pin and can be directly connected to VIN", EN abs max 35 V), and the 100k is series protection plus a place to add the Eq.1/Eq.2 UVLO divider or the Eq.3 start-delay cap later. **Operating point** at VIN 5.0 V / VOUT 3.3 V / 1.1 MHz, Eq.7: dIL = 3.3 x 1.7 / (5.0 x 4.7u x 1.1M) = **0.22 A pk-pk** (0.27 A at the -20 % inductance limit), so Eq.8 gives a **0.41 A** inductor peak at the 0.3 A peak rail load. On USB, VIN falls to ~4.5 V behind D1 and dIL drops to 0.17 A. **L2** is CJIANG **FTC404030S4R7MGCA** (LCSC C39676083, the inductor the removed AP63205 stage used as L3): 4.7 uH ±20 % at 1 MHz/1.0 Vrms, DCR 41 mOhm typ / 46 mOhm max, Irms 4.3 A typ / 4.0 A worst case (40 °C rise), Isat 7.0 A typ / 6.0 A worst case (30 % drop), 4.1 x 4.1 x 3.0 mm molded metal-composite, shielded (SZ CJIANG FTC series datasheet Rev 7.0 2025/11/05), on the vendored `L_Changjiang_FTC404030S` footprint. Against a 0.41 A peak that is a 10x margin — enormously over-rated for this rail, and deliberately so: it is already on the BOM and on the reel, and it costs 46 mOhm x 0.3 A² ≈ 4 mW. If the 4 x 4 x 3.0 mm land ever has to shrink, the FTC303020D family (3 x 3 x 2.0 mm, LCSC C7423318 at 2.2 uH) is the same footprint family and 2.2 uH is still inside the datasheet's 2.2-10 uH window, at 0.47 A pk-pk ripple. **Derating caveat**: C8 and C10/C11 are the datasheet *nominal* values and ceramic DC-bias derating is not in them — a 22 uF / 10 V 0603 at 3.3 V keeps roughly 60 %, so the 44 uF of COUT is ~26 uF effective.

U12 TPS7A2033PDBVR (fixed 3.3 V, active discharge), **C25 1 uF 0402 in / C26 2.2 uF 16 V X5R 0603 out**, **EN tied to V3V3_SYS itself**. The output value is a datasheet floor, not a nameplate one: the TPS7A20 is stable with a 1 uF minimum ceramic output capacitor, and Sec 7.1.1 says the recommended values already assume "the effective capacitance to decrease by as much as 50 %" under DC bias — so the 1 uF is an **effective** figure. A 1 uF 0402 biased at 3.3 V has no headroom left against it, and **2.2 uF / 16 V X5R 0603 (LCSC C23630, JLC Basic) is the smallest part on the JLC Basic library that still meets the floor after that derating**; C25 stays a 1 uF 0402 at the input, where the datasheet asks only for "at least equal to the nominal value" and nothing depends on it. The AP63203 has no power-good output, so the rail does the buck-before-LDO sequencing instead of a status pin, at no parts. What is lost against the old PWR_GOOD arrangement is the *"in regulation"* qualifier: EN now releases at U12's own ~0.9 V enable threshold on a rising V3V3_SYS, not at the point a comparator declares the buck settled. For a 10-20 mA sensor rail behind a 4 ms soft-start that is a few hundred microseconds, and firmware holds the sensors in reset for far longer.

**C19, the 5V_IN bulk, is 100 uF / 6.3 V polymer, ESR <= 40 mOhm** (Panasonic 6TPE100MAZB or a KEMET T520/T530 B case) in `Capacitor_Tantalum_SMD:CP_EIA-3528-21_Kemet-B`. It **moved from V5_SYS to 5V_IN** with the BEC decision: hold-up belongs on the rail the servo and module rows are on, which is now ahead of the OR-ing FET. V5_SYS keeps only C8 at the buck input. 6.3 V on a 5.0 V rail is the normal 1.26x polymer derating; a tantalum electrolytic would need 2x and would not qualify.

**The 5 V OR (Q1 + D1).** Q1 **AO3401A** (AOS, SOT-23, LCSC C15127, JLC **Basic**): VDS -30 V, ID -4.0 A, RDS(on) 60 mOhm max at VGS = -4.5 V / 85 mOhm max at -2.5 V, VGS(th) -0.45 to -1.0 V. Drain on `5V_IN`, source on `V5_SYS`, gate on `USB_VBUS` with **R7 100k** to GND. A P-channel body diode points drain -> source, i.e. 5V_IN -> V5_SYS, which is the direction the load is in, so the BEC gets through even before the channel is enhanced. **No USB**: R7 (helped by the R29/R30 VBUS_SENSE divider, 25k to GND) holds the gate at 0 V, VGS = -V5_SYS ≈ -5 V, the channel is hard on and the BEC carries both regulators through <= 60 mOhm. **USB present**: the gate sits at VBUS, VGS = VBUS - V5_SYS >= 0 and the channel is **off** — the one guarantee this OR has to make. D1 **1N5819WS** (SOD-323, LCSC C191023, JLC **Basic**): anode `USB_VBUS`, cathode `V5_SYS`, 40 V / 1 A Schottky, VF ~0.45 V at the ~250 mA V5_SYS draws on USB, so **V5_SYS is ~4.4-4.6 V from a 5 V host** — above the AP63203's 3.8 V minimum input with margin — and the diode blocks the reverse direction Q1 no longer has to. With both sources present the BEC wins whenever 5V_IN - I·RDS(on) exceeds VBUS - VF, which at these currents it always does, and Q1 is off in that state, so nothing flows back into the host either way. **What is given up** against the TPS2121 this replaces: no priority comparator, no input over-voltage lockout, no current limit, no soft-start, no open-drain status pin (**GPIO37 is a plain spare again**) and no seamless make-before-break — the crossover is a diode, not a 100 us controlled switchover. Two JLC Basic parts instead of one Extended IC plus six resistors and three capacitors.

Modelled conversion loss (simulations/README.md): buck ~0.09 W at 300 mA, ~0.14 W at 500 mA (92% assumed efficiency), LDO ~0.02 W. **These decks model the pre-2026-09-17 power tree** (on-board AP63205 pack buck -> TPS2121 mux -> TPS62913 two-stage 3.3 V buck) and have not been re-derived for the BEC + Q1/D1 + AP63203 tree, so they are history rather than evidence about the current design — see "Open items". Treat 500 mA as a burst ceiling until the real enclosure is measured.

### Battery monitoring

VBAT_SENSE (ADC0 / GPIO40, R27/R28 100k/10k, 36.3 V full scale, ~8.9 mV/LSB at 12 bit — a one-point calibration against a bench meter gets per-cell voltage well under 0.1 V error) plus the ESC's CURR output (ADC2 / GPIO42, 12.75 mV/A through R53/C78, ~0.063 A/LSB, integrated in firmware for mAh consumed) **are** the battery monitor. An INA226 (or any shunt-based coulomb counter) was rejected: it needs a shunt resistor in the pack's B+ or B- line, and this board deliberately has no such pass-through — the pack never passes through this board at all now — VBAT arrives on J3 pad 9 as a sense wire into a 100k divider, so there is no series element to add drop or a fault point on a 2-6S propulsion line. Low-battery threshold is a firmware decision (per-cell voltage, since cell count is not sensed), not a board one.

## Physical design

Placement and routing are not done; this section is the brief that placement has to satisfy.

### Envelope and stack-up

Rectangular board, **50.7 x 45.5 mm** (delivered by `tools/setup_pcb.py`, defaults `BOARD_W`/`BOARD_H`; area 2307 mm2; front courtyard sum 1334 mm2 = **57.8 % fill**, back 181 mm2 = **7.9 %**; Edge.Cuts bounding box 50.80 x 45.60 mm at 0.05 mm outline width) — grown back out from the previous **46.6 x 41.2 mm** (1920 mm2; the same ~1334 mm2 of front courtyard read as **69.5 % fill** at that size, back 9.4 %, after the JLC Basic-part refactor moved every resistor from 0201 to 0402 and ceramics to 0603/0805; Edge.Cuts 46.70 x 41.30 mm). That 46.6 x 41.2 mm figure was itself a shrink from the madflight FC3v2 envelope (50.7 x 41.6 mm) to the smallest size that passed every placement rule — 40.0 mm and 39.0 mm heights were tried along the way and both failed on fit/geometry — and, one revision earlier still, moving the ESC pad row J3, the DBG pad row J10 and TP1-TP10 to the **back** (see "Back side" below) is what let it shrink at all, from an even earlier 48.7 x 41.2 mm front-only envelope. At 46.6 x 41.2 mm, after the JLC refactor, the packer placed with **0 fit problems but 31 satellite-distance check failures** — decoupling/pull-up parts beyond their rank limits around U7, U20, U26, U25, J11 — so the board grew back out rather than staying shrunk: 50.7 mm restores the madflight FC3v2 width, and 45.5 mm adds 4.3 mm of height on top of that. **After the BEC revision** (DESIGN_SPEC "Decisions", 2026-09-17) the part count fell from 140 to 116 — and to **106** after the decoupling revision below — and the front courtyard fill from 57.8 % to **52.1 %** (1203 mm2 of 2307, back 189 mm2 = 8.2 %): the on-board pack buck, the TPS2121 mux, the TPS62913 second stage and the USB ESD array all left, and the whole power chain moved into the left-centre pocket those parts vacated. The top band above the MCU — the tightest neighbourhood on the board, and the subject of most of the history below including the half millimetre of board height and the `U7_SEP`/`U7_LANE` constants (both now deleted) — is empty. The board is kept at 50.7 x 45.5 mm rather than re-shrunk: re-shrinking is a separate exercise against the same placement rules. The generator places with **0 fit problems and 0 geometry problems** at that size, and everything in the rest of this paragraph is the history of how it got there. At 50.7 x 45.5 mm the generator previously placed with **0 fit problems and 0 geometry problems** — every placement rule passes at the same limits as before (SAT_NEAR_MM ranks 3.0/4.0/6.0/10.0 mm, CAP_NEAR_MM 6.0 mm, and every other rank, clearance, keepout and check unrelaxed), and the dry-run is deterministic: repeat runs are byte-identical. The 0.5 mm over the previously-tried 45.0 mm is what it took to seat the one part that would not fit even under the rewritten packer below: **C9**, U7's second 10 uF VIN cap, rank 0, limit 3.0 mm. U7's pocket is walled by the microSD socket on the left, the H2 grommet keepout on the right and the board edge above, and the strip above U7 — already carrying C13, R7, R8, R9 and C8 — was only 2.25 mm wide. Hanging the U7/L2 band 5.0 mm below the top edge instead of 4.0 (`band_y = HY - 5.0`, was `HY - 4.0`) widens that strip to 3.25 mm so C9 can sit on its side, and that only works with the extra board height: 45.5 mm with the old `HY - 4.0` band still comes up one part short. C9 now sits at 2.91 mm of its 3.0 mm limit and R8 at 3.89 of 4.0 — the two thinnest margins on the board, and the first place to check if U7's band, `U7_SEP`/`U7_LANE`, the microSD socket or the H2 keepout ever move again.

What actually cleared **C44**, **L20** and **C80** — the other three checks that used to overrun their rank limits around U20/U26 — was not the extra height but a rewrite of the satellite packer (`sat_cands`/`sat_assign`/`_sat_pack`/`sat_round`/`place_satellites` in `tools/setup_pcb.py`). The closed satellite passes no longer place one part at a time, nearest-first: every legal position of every satellite in a class is enumerated (same lattice, seeds, clearances and per-rank limit as before), the parts are split into independent neighbourhoods by candidate-hull overlap, and each neighbourhood is solved as one arrangement — depth-first, rank first, then fewest-surviving-positions first, nearest-first within a part, with backtracking; rotation is searched as a candidate (all four, symmetric pairs collapsed), not used as a tie-break. Parts still missing their limit after that pass are pinned and the table re-solved, up to `SAT_ROUNDS = 4` rounds, keeping the best result; `SAT_CANDS = 80` positions per part and `SAT_NODES = 4000` per neighbourhood are effort knobs, not rules — on budget exhaustion the search degrades to the old nearest-first order rather than failing outright. With coordination, all seven of U7's rank-0 caps seat: the old "6 slots for 7 parts" reading of U7's pocket was the previous packer's greedy order failing, not a real capacity limit. At the old 46.6 x 41.2 mm size the new packer scores **0 fit problems and 18 geometry problems** (down from 31 under the old packer) — history, not a size under consideration. The earlier floorplan changes — the H2/H4/H3 keepout-circle clamps, `U7_SEP` 5.5, `U7_LANE` 1.0, J11 at `HY - 1.6`, `xtal_y` +9.2, `u25_x` 11.95, `SAT_TANGENT` — all stand unchanged. The interior stays right-edge-anchored (`mcu_x = HX - 24.55`), so the new width lands in the west pocket (U25/U26/L3) and the QFN's left flank — the crowded regions — and the new height goes to the U7/L2 band under the microSD (L2/U7 band lowered to `HY - 5.0`, L2-to-U7 separation 4.5 -> 5.5 mm, with a 1.0 mm lane kept between U7 and the H2 keepout circle) and to the band above J11's pad row (socket anchored at `HY - 1.6` instead of `HY - 0.6` so the SD pull-ups have room). Three groups that used to slide into the grommet keepouts as the board grew — U7/L2 vs H2, U8 and the buck row vs H4, C62/C63 on the back vs H3 — are now solved explicitly against the keepout circles, verified clean from 46.6 x 41.2 up to 52 x 46. The satellite seed now biases end-of-flank pads toward the flank ends (`SAT_TANGENT`). The crystal moved 1 mm further above the QFN (`xtal_y = mcu_y + 9.2`), and U25 moved one 0402 column further from the QFN's left flank. The mounting square is **off-centre in X**: with nothing left on the front outboard of the left holes, the left margin is now just what the grommet keepout itself needs — the standard 30.5 mm pitch sits **4.0 mm in from the left edge** (Ø6.5 mm grommet-flange courtyard, 3.25 mm radius, plus 0.75 mm to the board edge) and (50.7 - 4.0 - 30.5 =) **16.2 mm in from the right edge** — the wider margin on the right is where the 3-column IO block sits, clear of the grommet keepouts. Holes are centred in Y. Passives are **0402 (every resistor, and ceramics <= 4.7 uF at <= 16 V, and the 50 V 100 nF and 18 pF parts) / 0603 (10-22 uF at 10 V) / 0805 (22 uF at 25 V, 10 uF at 50 V, 47 uF at 6.3 V)**. Resistors moved from 0201 to 0402: JLCPCB's Basic library has no 0201 resistor of any value, so every 0201 resistor line carried a per-unique-part Extended setup fee, and the U7 FB divider's 0.1 % values do not exist in 0201 at JLC at all — 0402 makes 8 of the 13 resistor values Basic and is hand-reworkable, which 0201 is not (reports/jlc-audit.md sections 2 and 6). C46/C47 moved from 15 pF to 18 pF (see the MCU sheet entry) to match Y1's new CL 12 pF crystal. The polymer bulk cap C19 is now an EIA-3528 (B) case, down from EIA-7343 (D). **0402 is not hand-reworkable** — nor is a 22 uF 0603 — and that is the deliberate trade for this envelope: JLC places both in assembly, a bench iron does not rework them, so a mis-stuffed board is a re-order, not a repair. (Every resistor was 0201 before this revision moved the whole set to 0402 for JLC stocking reasons, above; 0402 is smaller than 0603 but no easier to hand-rework than 0201 was.) **4-layer SIG-GND-PWR-SIG, 1.6 mm**, JLC-standard stack-up. Maximum component height **4.4 mm above the top surface** — the tallest parts today are L3 at 3.1 mm and the USB-C receptacle at ~4.2 mm (check against the real drawing); L2 is 2.0 mm tall (CJIANG FTC303020D, the same 3.0 x 3.0 x 2.0 mm envelope the board was laid out against). **Single-sided assembly: every component that is fitted is on F.Cu.** B.Cu carries routing, pads and unpopulated (DNP) lands only, so the board can be reflowed once and sits flat on the ESC below it — the bare-pad exception this permits is exactly what J3, J10 and TP1-TP10 use, and the DNP exception is exactly what the U24/C62/C63 expansion land uses; see "Back side" below. Weight is not constrained.

### Mounting and orientation

Standard **30.5 x 30.5 mm** four-hole pattern, off-centre in X (see "Envelope and stack-up"): the left holes sit **4.0 mm in from the left edge** — the Ø6.5 mm grommet-flange courtyard (3.25 mm radius) plus 0.75 mm to the board edge — and the right holes follow at the 30.5 mm pitch, 16.2 mm in from the right edge; both pairs are centred in Y. Each hole is a **Ø4.0 mm plated through hole** for a Ø3 mm silicone grommet — the finished hole is unchanged; what changed is that it is now **grounded**, carrying a **Ø6.4 mm GND pad ring** on all four layers stitched by **eight Ø0.6 mm / Ø0.3 mm vias on a 2.7 mm radius**, so each frame screw bonds to the ground plane instead of sitting in a copper hole. The ring replaces the Ø5.0 mm copper-free annulus of the previous revision: the three GND pours now flood into the hole and connect to it solidly, and what the back-side bare pads — J3, J10, TP1-TP10, see "Back side" — have to clear is the **ring plus the 0.15 mm copper clearance, Ø6.7 mm** (`MOUNT_COPPER_R` in `tools/setup_pcb.py`), still an all-layer rule and not just a front-side one. On top of that sits the unchanged **Ø6.5 mm top-side courtyard keepout** for the grommet flange (footprint `MARV_Packages:MountingHole_4.0mm_Grommet_Pad_Via`, symbols H1-H4 on the io_block sheet, pin 1 of each on GND). The keepout was Ø8.0 mm before this revision; madflight's own mounting pins sit only about 2.3 mm from the hole edge, so the extra clearance in an 8 mm courtyard was buying nothing the flange itself needs — it was costing ~68 mm2 of placeable area across the four holes, and shrinking it to Ø6.5 mm (matching the flange, not the flange plus a hand-clearance margin) is part of what makes the smaller envelope above fit. Board orientation: the **front edge is the bottom edge (-Y)** and it faces the ESC's pad row when the two boards are stacked in the same orientation.

### Connection philosophy

Carried over from MARV V1.2 and madflight practice, revised for the 3-column IO block: **solder pads only for the ESC row (J3) and the DBG landing (J10)** — the two ports that mate with something fixed and pad-shaped (the ESC's own pads, a debug probe). **Every other external signal — GPS, ELRS, magnetometer, PWM5-8 and every exposed spare GPIO — leaves on the 2.54 mm J6/J7/J8 IO block**, madflight FC3v2 style: each row is a signal pin (J6, innermost column, next to the MCU) with its own power pin (J7, middle column) and its own GND pin (J8, outermost column, at the board edge) directly beside it, so a 3-wire module lead or a servo plug goes straight across one row of the one block instead of needing its own connector. There is no separate servo header any more — PWM5-8 share this same block, on the same footing as GPS/ELRS/MAG. No JST-GH, no pin headers for buses. The two solder-pad connectors (J3, J10) share one footprint family — SMD pads **1.4 x 2.2 mm oval on 2.00 mm pitch**, authored on F.Cu/F.Mask and **placed flipped to the back (B.Cu/B.Mask)** by `tools/setup_pcb.py` — see "Back side" — with a silkscreen label per pad, mirrored text on B.SilkS, added at layout, and a pin-1 mark. **Row order must match the mating device**, which is why J3 is defined by the ESC silkscreen rather than by any convention of ours.

### Back side

`J3`, `J10`, TP1-TP10 and the optional expansion socket `U24` are on **B.Cu**, not F.Cu. Assembly stays single-sided — **nothing on the back is ever reflowed**: the two solder-pad rows and the ten test points are bare pads, and U24 is **DNP** (`(dnp yes)` + `(in_bom no)` in the schematic, so it is not bought, not placed and not in `power_bom.csv` as a fitted part). The decoupling caps `C62` and `C63` are fitted on the front (F.Cu). All of them are authored like every other footprint on F.Cu and then placed **flipped** by `tools/setup_pcb.py`, which mirrors pads, mask, silkscreen and courtyard onto the matching B.* layers (KiCad's own layer bookkeeping on flip — see LIBRARIES.md's pad-row and test-point notes). This is exactly the exception `"B.Cu carries routing, pads and unpopulated lands only"` (see "Envelope and stack-up") already allows; the board is still reflowed once and still sits flat on the ESC below it. `tools/setup_pcb.py`'s `check_back()` asserts both halves of that rule — exactly which refs are flipped, **and** that every flipped part which is not a pad row or a test point really does carry the DNP attribute — so a socket that silently became a populated back-side component fails the build.

**Why these move to the back:** the board stacks directly on top of the ESC, back side down, so J3 facing the ESC lets the 8-wire harness run straight down between two fixed, order-matched pad rows instead of around an edge; J10 and TP1-TP10 are bench-only connections, read with the stack pulled apart, so their reachability does not depend on which side of the board they are on. Moving those three off the front is what let the envelope shrink from 48.7 x 41.2 mm to 46.6 x 41.2 mm (see "Envelope and stack-up"): it freed the 7 mm left-edge pocket J3 used to need, the bottom-right corner J10 used to need, and the ten 1 mm pads TP1-TP10 used to take out of the sensor island. The QSPI socket followed for a different reason — it is an **option**, not a feature, and an unpopulated land costs nothing on the back while the 7.5 x 5.5 mm it used to occupy under the MCU on the front is what the MCU decoupling and the U7 output caps got back. The cost is on silkscreen: the front loses every board-level silk label except the IO block's, which now sit in a fixed reserved band (see "IO block (J6/J7/J8)"); every back-side reference designator and pad label is mirrored text on B.SilkS instead, readable when the board is viewed from the back.

**The QSPI expansion socket (U24) and its bypass capacitor (C62).** A 150-mil SOIC-8 land against the MCU's QSPI pads 70-75, which face down because U20 is at 180°, so the six QSPI nets drop straight through the board and every stub is one via plus the 1.6 mm board thickness. **C62 (1 uF) is fitted on the front and is the whole bypass** so a user who adds a chip only has to solder the socket itself. 1 uF, not the 100 nF it was: the APS6404L PSRAM option **requires** a low-ESR 1 uF at VDD (its datasheet Sec 16.4, which calls the 0.1 uF the *optional* one) while the W25Q32JV flash datasheet names no supply capacitor at all, so one 1 uF covers both fits and **the land is built PSRAM-ready** — nothing has to be swapped when the socket is populated. The C63 1 uF that used to sit beside C62 is removed: it is the same capacitor as C62 now, not a second one. The back-side silk legend is minimal: **`FLASH` / `SOIC-8`**, plus an explicit `1` pin-1 marker beside pin 1. **Compatible parts for the U24 socket:** any 3.3 V QSPI NOR flash or PSRAM in SOIC-8 150 mil (3.9 mm body) with the standard pinout (CS# DO WP# GND DI CLK HOLD# VCC). Every part below was checked against its own datasheet or its LCSC package listing for the 150 mil body — package codes in these families are **not** interchangeable by capacity or by a similar-looking suffix, which is exactly what put the wrong part in this list before (see the W25Q64JVSSIQ note in "Back side" above). Both families share the socket; only one part can be fitted:
- **Flash (firmware currently expects this for logging if a chip is fitted):** Winbond W25Q16JVSNIQ / W25Q32JVSNIQ / W25Q64JVSNIQ — the **`SN`** suffix is the 150 mil package across this whole family; the **`SS`** suffix looks similar but is SOIC-8 **208 mil** on every density (confirmed against LCSC's package field for W25Q16JVSSIQ and W25Q32JVSSIQ, not just the W25Q64JVSSIQ the earlier revision named), so W25Q16JVSSIQ / W25Q32JVSSIQ / W25Q64JVSSIQ are all dropped from this list, not just the one part found before. W25Q128JVSIQ is 208 mil and does NOT fit either. GigaDevice GD25Q32C, ordered in its **SOP8-150MIL** package option (datasheet section "Package SOP8 150MIL"; the same die is also sold as VSOP8-208MIL, so the package must be specified at order time — GD25Q64C is dropped from this list because no 150 mil package option could be confirmed for it, only 208 mil). Macronix MX25L3233F, ordered in its **8-pin SOP (150mil)** package option (the same die is also sold as SOP 200mil) — MX25L6433F is dropped from this list because its own datasheet's package section lists only SOP-8 200mil, no 150 mil option.
- **PSRAM:** APS6404L-3SQR-SN / ESP-PSRAM64H / IPS6404L-SQ / LY68L6400 (SOP-8 150 mil confirmed for each — ESP-PSRAM64H via its JLC package listing `SOP-8_3.9x4.9x1.27P`, IPS6404L-SQ and LY68L6400 via their own datasheets — same pinout as the flash family).

**R35, the FLASH_CS1 10k pull-up, is NOT DNP** and stays on the front: GPIO0 has to be held deselected whether or not the socket is fitted. The nets are unchanged — `tools/check_power_netlist.py` still checks all eight U24 pins against QSPI_SCLK/SD0-SD3/FLASH_CS1/V3V3_SYS/GND — and it additionally asserts that U24 carries `dnp` + `exclude_from_bom` in the netlist, that nothing else on the board does, and that R35 and C62 do not.

## Decisions

- **5 V-in from an external BEC, not pack-powered (reverses the earlier decision, 2026-09-17)**: the board takes regulated 5 V on the ESC pad row (J3 pad 7, `5V_IN`) from a BEC wired between the ESC's VBAT pad and the FC; the on-board AP63205WU pack buck (U26, L3, C73-C77, C80, R52) is removed. Rationale: this was the owner's stated requirement and it is the more flexible architecture, not the less: any 2-6S BEC or a 1S boost module works upstream without touching the board, and the complex, hot, high-voltage stage lives off-board where it can be swapped. The earlier reversal ("the AM32 ESC has no BEC, so make 5 V on the board") was a design change made without asking and is withdrawn. Raw pack now reaches the FC only as a sense wire: J3 gains a ninth pad `VBAT` that feeds the R27/R28/C48 divider (GPIO41) and nothing else; `VBAT` carries no load and gets no copper zone.
- **USB / 5 V OR: P-FET + Schottky, not a TPS2121 mux**: Q1 AO3401A (drain `5V_IN`, source `V5_SYS`, gate `USB_VBUS` with a 100 k pull-down) and D1 1N5819WS (`USB_VBUS` -> `V5_SYS`). USB present turns the FET off, so the host can never back-feed the BEC/servo net; BEC present with no USB turns it fully on (body diode lifts the source, then the channel carries the load). Both parts are JLC Basic. U25 TPS2121 and its R42-R47/C70-C72 are removed; `PWR_SRC_ST` is dropped and GPIO37 is freed (USB presence is already readable via the VBUS_SENSE divider). The IO-block 5 V rows (1-4, 7-10) move from `V5_SYS` to `5V_IN`, i.e. ahead of the OR, so servo current never passes through the FET and USB never sees the servo rows; `V5_SYS` feeds only the two 3.3 V regulators. C19 (100 uF polymer) sits on `5V_IN` at the pad row.
- **PWM1-4 to the ESC pads, PWM5-8 to the IO block**: the four motor signals go straight to the stacked ESC on solder pads, so no motor signal leaves the stack on a connector; the remaining four PWM channels for gimbals/airbrakes/deployment share the IO block (rows 7-10) with every other external signal instead of a dedicated servo header.
- **WS2812C-2020 instead of two discrete LEDs**: one 2 x 2 mm addressable pixel replaces D20/D21 + R31/R32, frees GPIO27, and its 2.7 V DIN input threshold works from 3.3 V CMOS (a 5050 WS2812B needs 0.7 x VDD = 3.5 V and would not). Costs one timing-critical GPIO instead of two plain ones.
- **3.3 V system rail: fixed-output AP63203WU-7 (JLC Extended), not the TPS62913 stage and not an LDO**: a simple fixed 3.3 V / 2 A buck with datasheet inductor and ceramics (~8 parts) replaces U7 TPS62913 + L2 + FB1 + 0.1 % divider + two-stage output filter (~25 parts). The V3V3_SYS load is ~180 mA typical / ~300 mA peak (MCU + SD; GPS/ELRS are on the 5 V rows), which a linear regulator would turn into 0.3-0.5 W of heat at 5 V in: the owner has already had an AMS1117 overheat on this rail, so the switching stage stays. Its 3.8 V minimum input covers the USB path (5 V minus the Schottky drop, ~4.4 V) with margin. JLC's Basic library contains no synchronous buck at all (API search, 2026-09-17: 197 hits, all Extended), so the Extended loading fee is accepted for this one part. `PWR_GOOD` is dropped; U12's EN ties to `V3V3_SYS`, which gives the same buck-before-LDO ordering with no parts.
- **Split V3V3_SYS (buck) / V3V3_ANA (LDO), kept**: the microSD and MCU stay on the buck; U12 TPS7A2033 (Extended, kept deliberately for its noise floor) carries only the MEMS sensors and ADC_AVDD at ~10-20 mA, so its dissipation is negligible and the sensor rail stays quiet.
- **USB ESD array removed**: U8 USBLC6-2SC6 is JLC Extended; per the owner's rule (Extended -> remove unless it earns its fee) it is dropped and D+/D- go straight to the 27 R series resistors, as on the madflight FC3v2.
- **ICM-45686 replaces the BMI088**: a single-chip-select 6-axis IMU (one AP_CS, one AP_SDO on SENS_MISO) simplifies the sensor bus to one pull-up and one interrupt pair per part instead of BMI088's separate accelerometer/gyroscope CSB and SDO lines; see LIBRARIES.md for the datasheet-derived pin map.
- **GPIO0 as second QSPI chip select (QMI CS1) for the log flash**: all six QSPI pads are bonded out on RP2354B and GPIO0/8/19/47 can serve as QMI CS1 per Raspberry Pi "Hardware design with RP2350" SS3.1-3.2; QSPI_SS is reserved for BOOTSEL and cannot be reused as a second CS.
- **The QSPI socket becomes an optional back-side expansion (U24 DNP, its C62 1 uF bypass fitted)**: the RP2354B already carries 2 MB of in-package flash and the board already carries a microSD for logs, so a second QSPI device is a *want*, not a requirement — and paying for socket and placement on every board is the wrong default. The socket is marked DNP with the land on B.Cu against the MCU's QSPI pads, but the bypass capacitor is fitted on the front so a user who adds a chip only has to solder the socket. It costs nothing but copper and a silk legend, and a user who wants 8 MB of NOR or a PSRAM hand-solders one SOIC-8. Consequences: the 25 mA flash burst leaves the V3V3_SYS budget; the nets, the pin checks and R35 are unchanged; the front pocket under the MCU goes to the MCU decoupling; and "single-sided assembly" now means "nothing fitted is on the back" rather than "nothing at all is on the back", which `check_back()` enforces by asserting the DNP attribute rather than trusting the placement table.
- **C19 100 uF / 6.3 V polymer (EIA-3528 B) rather than 220 uF / 10 V (EIA-7343 D)**: C19 is the V5_SYS hold-up across the TPS2121's 100 us switchover, so this is a behavioural change, not a package change. Both decks were re-run at 100 uF nominal / 80 uF derated and every predicate still passes with ~0.9 V of margin to the 3.5 V V5_SYS floor (worst case 4.374 V at 0.8 A; see "Open items"), which is what licenses the 43.6 -> 14.0 mm2 of courtyard it gives back. 6.3 V on a 5.0 V rail is the normal 1.26x polymer derating; a tantalum electrolytic would need 2x and would not qualify, so the ESR <= 40 mOhm / polymer wording is load-bearing, not a preference.
- **Decoupling is datasheet-only: one capacitor per supply pin, at the value that part's own datasheet names** (2026-09-17). Every supply pin on this board used to carry a 100 nF + 1 uF pair. That pair was a house style — nothing on the board cited a datasheet for the second capacitor — so it is replaced by a rule that can be checked against a PDF: fit what the part's datasheet asks for, and where a datasheet is silent fit 100 nF per pin as the minimum HF bypass **and say that is what it is**. Case by case: **ICM-45686** — Table 11, its own bill of materials, names 0.1 uF on VDD and 0.1 uF on VDDIO, so C50/C52 stay and C51/C53 go; **BMP581** — the datasheet specifies no supply capacitor at all, so C54/C56 stay as the board's minimum HF bypass, by this rule and not by the datasheet, and C55/C57 go; **ADXL375** — "Power Supply Decoupling" asks for 1 uF at VS and 0.1 uF at VDD I/O, the **opposite** pairing from the other two, so C59 (1 uF) and C60 (100 nF) stay and C58/C61 go; **the U24 socket land** — 1 uF, which is what the APS6404L PSRAM requires (Sec 16.4) and more than the W25Q32JV flash, silent on decoupling, asks for, so C62 becomes a 1 uF that covers both fits and C63 goes. The same rule removes **C9** (a 100 nF HF bypass at U7's VIN, beyond AP63203 Table 2), **C20/C21/C22** (local bulk at the IO block: C19 at the pad row and the buck's own output capacitors already cover those rows) and **C66** (the third microSD bulk capacitor). Twelve capacitors leave and the part count falls **116 -> 106**. What is given up is high-frequency bypass margin that no datasheet asked for: measure rail noise on hardware before any of it is added back (see "Open items").
- **A USB power indicator, D21 + R56**: `USB_VBUS` -> R56 1 k -> net `USB_LED_A` -> D21 anode, cathode to GND, so it lights only with a host plugged in and is dark on BEC-only power. That is the question a bench user asks of this connector and the one D20 cannot answer, because the RGB pixel runs off V5_SYS and lights on either source. D21 is a **white** 0603 (KT-0603W, LCSC C2290, JLC **Basic**), not blue: JLC stocks no Basic blue 0603 LED and a colour is not worth a per-unique-part Extended setup fee on an indicator. At VBUS 5.0 V and VF ~2.9 V the current is ~2.1 mA, about 40 % of the datasheet's 5 mA test current — dim by indicator standards, deliberately: R56 is the 1 k line the board already buys (LCSC C11702, shared with R21/R24/R53/R54) and 2 mA off USB costs the host nothing. It says **"VBUS is present"**, not "the regulators are up". A front-side `USB` F.SilkS legend beside the LED says which rail it watches.

## Heat and interference

- Enclosed, no forced airflow; qualification ambient 60 C; junctions below 100 C; exposed-pad vias and ground copper are the heat path.
- 4-layer SIG-GND-PWR-SIG: In1.Cu is a solid GND plane, In2.Cu carries the V3V3_SYS pour with the V3V3_ANA island keyholed out of it, and F.Cu/B.Cu are GND pours. **There is no VBAT zone** — VBAT is a sense wire to a 100k divider now — and none of 5V_IN / V5_SYS / USB_VBUS gets a zone either: they are short, high-current *nets* in one corner of the board and belong on wide tracks in the Power net class (0.50 mm track, 0.15 mm clearance), not on a plane layer that In2 needs for the 3.3 V rail. **The whole power chain is in the left-centre pocket**, left to right: Q1 (with C19, its 5V_IN hold-up) nearest the left edge, where 5V_IN arrives on the back at J3 pad 7; then U7; then L2 on U7's SW/BST side. That puts the one switching node on the board ~18.6 mm from the nearest MEMS package, against an 8.0 mm rule. U12 and the sensors are on the ground-referenced analog island near the centre, away from the switcher, the ESC row and any inductor; no inductor under the IMU/barometer; servo and ESC return currents off the sensor ground; magnetometer external, away from power leads and the ESC. The board sits directly above a 4-in-1 ESC switching motor currents, so the IMU cluster's ground reference and the 5V_IN loop are the two things that decide whether the sensors are usable.

## Manufacturing (JLCPCB)

The board is designed against JLCPCB's combined PCB + SMT assembly service. A full parts audit against JLC's live catalogue, a per-board cost/setup-fee estimate and the fabrication quote parameter sheet are in **`reports/jlc-audit.md`** — that report is not duplicated here because its numbers move every time a part changes; this section is the summary that stays stable.

- **Assembly is single-sided, top only (F.Cu).** Every fitted SMT part is on the front. The back (B.Cu) carries only bare solder pads (J3 ESC row, J10 DBG row, TP1-TP10) and the **unpopulated (DNP) U24 socket land** — nothing on the back is ever reflowed, so the board is reflowed once and sits flat on the ESC below it in the stack. See "Back side".
- **Fabrication: 4-layer, 1.6 mm, stack-up JLC04161H-7628** (F.Cu 0.035 / PP-7628 0.2104 mm Er 4.4 / In1.Cu 0.0152 / FR4 core 1.065 mm Er 4.6 / In2.Cu 0.0152 / PP-7628 0.2104 / B.Cu 0.035 mm, already in the board file). Select this stack-up explicitly at order time — it is not JLC's 4-layer default, and the inner-layer spacing is what the USB D+/D- differential pair was drawn against.
- **Surface finish: ENIG, not HASL** (`copper_finish "ENIG"` already set in the board). U20 is a QFN-80 on 0.4 mm pitch with a 3.4 x 3.4 mm exposed pad, and U21/U22/U23 are LGA parts down to 0.5 mm pitch with no visible, inspectable, reworkable joint; HASL's domed, pad-to-pad-uneven finish is a bridging/coplanarity risk under the QFN and a non-wet risk under the LGAs that optical inspection will not catch. The 0402 passives want a flat finish too.
- **Fab rules used**: min track/space 0.127 mm (5 mil), min via 0.2 mm drill / 0.45 mm pad, min annular ring 0.1 mm, copper-to-edge 0.3 mm, hole-to-hole 0.254 mm — all inside JLC's standard 4-layer capability, so none of these force an advanced-fab order. No impedance control ordered (the USB pair is designed by geometry against the stack-up instead — see reports/jlc-audit.md section 4.1 for the trade). No panelization (JLC panelizes small orders itself).
- **THT headers J6/J7/J8 (the 1x14 IO block) ship unpopulated**, "customer solders": JLC's hand-soldering service is priced per joint and per unique part, the three columns abut with no keep-out for hand insertion, and whether — and how — the block is populated is a per-airframe choice. Re-run the exporter with `--with-tht` if JLC is to solder them instead.
- **U24 is DNP**: the optional back-side QSPI flash/PSRAM socket carries `(dnp yes)` + `(in_bom no)` in the schematic, so it is excluded from both order files automatically; C62 (its 1 uF bypass) is fitted on the front regardless. See "Back side".
- **Every fitted symbol carries a hidden `LCSC` property**, stamped by `tools/build_power.py` from `tools/jlc/lcsc_map.csv` and carried into `reports/power-netlist.xml` by the ERC/netlist export. This is the number that gets ordered — not a value string re-parsed at export time.
- **Order files**: `python3 tools/jlc/export_jlc.py` (add `--with-tht` to have JLC solder J6/J7/J8) reads the netlist and the schematic's `LCSC` properties and writes `reports/jlc-bom.csv` (Comment, Designator, Footprint, LCSC Part #) and `reports/jlc-cpl.csv` (Designator, Mid X, Mid Y, Layer, Rotation) in JLC's own order-form format. The tool refuses to write a BOM with a missing LCSC number on any fitted, non-THT, non-DNP line.
- **33 unique LCSC parts, 16 Basic / 17 Extended**, over 37 unique fitted BOM lines, 323 fitted SMT joints, an extended-part setup-fee **estimate** of 17 x $3.00 = **$51.00** and **$39.39** of components per board at JLC's 10-piece tier (`python3 tools/jlc/audit.py`, run 2026-09-17 against the live catalogue; U24 is excluded as DNP and J6/J7/J8 as THT shipped unpopulated). **The full audit, the per-line table and the quote parameter sheet are in `reports/jlc-audit.md` sections 3-4**, along with the full quote parameter sheet (board size, layers, copper weights, silkscreen/mask colour, NPTH, etc.).
- **Gerbers do not exist yet.** The board was placed but unrouted (0 tracks / 0 vias / 0 zones) as of the audit. Export with `kicad-cli pcb export gerbers` + `kicad-cli pcb export drill` once routing is done, using the drill/place origin so the CPL file above lines up with them.
- **CPL rotations are unverified against JLC's own convention.** They were cross-checked byte-for-byte against `kicad-cli pcb export pos --use-drill-file-origin` (0 mismatches over 120 components), which proves the exporter is faithful to KiCad's own angles — it does not prove those angles match JLC's zero-degree reference for a given package. Check every rotation in JLC's assembly preview before ordering, with particular attention to U20 (180 degrees in the design), U21/U22/U23, D20, J4, U12, U7, Q1, D1 and J11.

- PCB layout not done: see **Physical design** for the envelope, stack-up, mounting pattern and edge allocation that placement has to satisfy. Final board size is set at placement (the smallest square that fits). The VBAT and 5V_IN copper must be sized for the buck's 2 A plus the servo draw, not just the avionics budget.
- **Gerbers need routing.** The board is placed but unrouted (0 tracks / 0 vias / 0 zones); no Gerber/drill set can be produced until routing is done. See "Manufacturing (JLCPCB)".
- **CPL rotations need to be checked in JLC's assembly preview** before ordering — they are verified to be KiCad's own angles, not verified against JLC's zero-degree convention per package. See "Manufacturing (JLCPCB)".
- **L3's 20 V DC withstand is at its rating, not clear of it.** At a full 6S pack (25.2 V) L3 sees VIN-VOUT = 20.2 V, against the CJIANG FTC404030S4R7MGCA datasheet's Note 8 20 V DC withstand. Not close below 5S. Re-check against the datasheet if this is ever pushed higher, or if 6S operation at the top of charge needs headroom here.

The rest stay open. The buzzer and its driver are gone entirely — a buzzer is now a firmware-free integration choice on one of the four exposed spare IO block rows (11-14, GPIO44-47), not a board feature.

Three decks for the BEC + Q1/D1 + AP63203 tree now exist (`simulations/or_stage.cir`, `buck_3v3.cir`, `ldo_ana.cir`) and are driven by `tools/run_power_checks.py` with machine-checked predicates and negative controls; see `simulations/RESULTS.md` for the table, which currently reports **Status: FAIL** — the buck and LDO predicates pass, but three of the four OR-stage predicates do not, because Q1's gate net *is* USB_VBUS, so VGS equals D1's forward drop and the P-FET channel is held off whenever USB is present, leaving Q1's body diode to carry the BEC path.
- **J3 pad pitch is unconfirmed.** MicoAir publishes no pad drawing for the AM32 4-in-1 FC row; 2.00 mm is assumed. The pad *order* is authoritative. Measure the physical ESC before fab.
- **WS2812C VDD.** Its absolute maximum supply is 5.3 V. V5_SYS is either the BEC's 5 V through Q1 or USB VBUS minus D1's drop; the second is *safer* than before (a diode drop below VBUS), but the first is whatever the external BEC puts out, and there is now **no over-voltage lockout anywhere on the board**. A BEC set to 6 V would exceed the LED's absolute maximum and the AP63203 would not care. Acceptable risk, a BEC-selection instruction, or a reason to add a clamp — lead's call.

They were left on V5_SYS deliberately, so the status LED works on USB-only bench power; the literal reading of "V5_SYS feeds only the two 3.3 V regulators" would put them on 5V_IN and make the LED dark whenever the BEC is absent. - **Optional series resistors on the PWM outputs, not yet added** (applies to PWM1-4 on the ESC pads as well as PWM5-8 on the IO block).
