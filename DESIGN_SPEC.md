# MARV V2 design specification — revision P3 (5 V in, low-noise buck + analog LDO)

Current specification. History: P0 (raw-pack buck, four-way OR-ing, USB
limiter), P1 (flight-controller loads, peripheral rails), P2 (BEC-fed
buck-boost) are kept as backups/DESIGN_SPEC.before-*.md with matching
backups/pre-*/ schematic snapshots. POWER_*.md are trade-study history.

## Purpose and scope

Flight controller for rockets and drones. MCU **RP2354B** (QFN-80, in-package
flash). Onboard sensors: BMI088, BMP581, ADXL375. Logging: QSPI NOR flash and
a **latched (push-push) microSD socket**. External daughterboards on generic
ports: GPS (UART), ELRS radio (UART), magnetometer (I2C); they take care of
their own circuitry. Rule: the simplest form of what the user states; for
unspecified details, what flight controllers in the wild do.

P3 implements power, ports, MCU, sensors, logging and PWM outputs in the
schematic. Firmware and PCB layout are not done. Not for manufacture or
flight.

## Power inputs

| Interface | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| J3 5V_IN | 5 V, 3 A from an external 2S-6S BEC, or from the 1S configuration's BMS/boost module. Must stay below 5.5 V including transients. The ESC is 3S-6S; only its BEC output reaches the board. |
| J4 USB-C | USB 2.0 data and 5 V bench power; no PD; no on-board current limiter (standard FC practice); the startup budget is a firmware matter. |
| V5_SYS | OR-ed 5 V rail: J3 and J4 each through an LM66100 ideal diode (U3, U5; CE tied to VOUT), C8/C9 2 x 10 uF ceramic, C19 220 uF polymer. |

**Consequence of the P3 part choice**: the TPS62913 is a buck only (VIN
3-17 V). A bare 1S cell can no longer feed the board (the P2 buck-boost
allowed that). The 1S configuration must deliver 5 V through its BMS/boost
module. If a bare-cell input is wanted later, a boost path is a new decision.

## Rails

```
V5_SYS ─┬─ U7 TPS62913 (low-noise buck, ferrite-bead output filter) ─ V3V3_SYS
        │     RP2354B IOVDD, QSPI_IOVDD, VREG_VIN; QSPI flash; microSD;
        │     GPS, radio and magnetometer ports (J6/J7/J9)
        └─ U12 TPS7A20-3.3 (LDO) ───────────────────────────────────── V3V3_ANA
              BMI088, BMP581, ADXL375, RP2354B ADC_AVDD and VREG_AVDD
```

Rationale (user direction, adopted): flash and especially the SD card are
sources of supply transients, so they and the MCU digital domains stay on
the 3 A buck with generous local bulk (SD: 10-47 uF + 100 nF at the socket;
flash: 1 uF + 100 nF; MCU: 100 nF per supply pin group plus local bulk, all
on the MCU sheet). The TPS62913 is specified by TI at < 20 uV RMS noise and
< 10 uV RMS ripple with its ferrite-bead output filter, so the MCU is not on
a noisy generic buck. The LDO carries only the MEMS sensors and the RP2354B
analog pins (Raspberry Pi asks for minimal noise on VREG_AVDD and local
decoupling on ADC_AVDD): ~10-20 mA, so LDO dissipation at 1.7 V drop is
~20-35 mW. No LDO on any high-current rail.

| Rail | Budget | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V3V3_SYS | 500 mA continuous / 800 mA peak <= 10 ms (typical ~300 mA) | TPS62913 rated 3 A; datasheet reference values, see sheet notes |
| V3V3_ANA | <= 50 mA (TPS7A20 300 mA) | 100 nF at each ADC_AVDD / VREG_AVDD pin on the MCU sheet; a ferrite between LDO and MCU analog pins only if measurements justify it |
| Peripheral ports | J6 UART_GPS, J7 UART_ELRS, J9 I2C_MAG, 4-pin JST-GH: 3V3, GND, signal, signal; V3V3_SYS direct, 10 uF local | loads count in the V3V3_SYS budget |
| Servo / propulsion | external only | never from V3V3 or USB |

Values as generated (from the datasheets' 3.3 V reference circuits; details
in the power_3v3 sheet notes): U7 TPS62913RPUR, VQFN-HR-10 2 x 2 mm, 2.2 MHz
with triangle spread-spectrum (S-CONF 6.04k), L2 2.2 uH (XGL4030-222, 7 A),
3 x 22 uF first-stage output, bead BLE18PS080SN1 (8.5 Ohm, 4 mOhm) with
2 x 22 uF after it and FB sensed after the bead, divider 15.8k/4.99k giving
3.33 V nominal (3.30-3.38 V over VFB tolerance), NR/SS 470 nF, 2.2 nF VIN
bypass, PG pulled up to V3V3_SYS. U12 TPS7A2033PDBVR (fixed 3.3 V, active
discharge), 1 uF in / 1 uF out, EN tied to IN. Passive MPNs beyond the
inductor and bead remain open.

Sheets (all generated by tools/build_power.py, which overwrites them —
preserve manual edits first): POWER 1 power_sources (J3, J4, U3, U5, V5_SYS),
POWER 2 power_3v3 (U7, U12, U8), POWER 3 power_periph (J6/J7/J9), BOARD 1
mcu (U20 RP2354B, Y1, SW1 reset, SW2 BOOTSEL, J10 SWD, LEDs D20/D21, buzzer
BZ1/Q20, sense dividers), BOARD 2 sensors (U21 BMI088, U22 BMP581, U23
ADXL375), BOARD 3 storage (U24 W25Q128JVS, J11 microSD), BOARD 4 actuators
(J12 PWM1-8, J13 SERVO_5V = 5V_IN, J14 GND — three 1x8 rows; a single 3-row
footprint may replace them at layout).

| ELRS module (SX1262 TX +22 dBm + its MCU) | 60 mA | 170 mA | SX1262 DS |

Modelled conversion loss (RESULTS.md): buck ~0.09 W at 300 mA, ~0.14 W at
500 mA (92 % assumed), LDO ~0.02 W. Enclosure scenarios in RESULTS.md; treat
500 mA as a burst ceiling until the real enclosure is measured.

## Heat and interference

- Enclosed, no forced airflow; qualification ambient 60 C; junctions below
  100 C; exposed-pad vias and ground copper as the heat path.
- 4-layer SIG-GND-PWR-SIG: solid L2 ground; L3 pours for V5_SYS / V3V3_SYS
  with ground fill; U7 and its inductor at the J3/J4 edge, SW loop over L2
  ground, shielded inductor, FB sense after the bead routed away from SW; U12
  and the sensors at the far end on a ground-referenced analog island; no
  inductor under the IMU/barometer; servo/ESC return currents off the sensor
  ground; magnetometer external, away from power leads and the ESC.
- The TPS62913 noise/ripple figures are datasheet claims; verify on hardware.

## Source behaviour (LM66100, CE = VOUT)

- Natural voltage priority between 5V_IN and USB; near-equal sources share.
  Turn-on after VOUT < VIN - VON (80-250 mV) in 40 us; turn-off when
  VOUT > VIN + VOFF (0-80 mV) in 2 us; body diode 0.5-1.1 V while off.
- Handover between the two 5 V sources dips V5_SYS by at most the body-diode
  drop for ~36 us; the buck needs V5_SYS >= 3.5 V, which C19 guarantees with
  margin (RESULTS.md). C19 also absorbs SD write transients at the input.
- Reverse current below VOFF/RON (~0.7 A at 5 V) is not blocked; a stiff
  source lifts V5_SYS past the trip point in microseconds. With both inputs
  at 5 V and no cell, no trickle-charge exposure remains.
- Insert inrush into a discharged V5_SYS is source-impedance-limited
  (~19 A peak modelled at 0.2 Ohm, lengthened by C19); the BEC/BMS module's
  own protection and the LM66100 pulse rating are bench-check items.

## Board stage (P3-B): MCU, sensors, logging, actuators — decisions

Principle confirmed by the user: the PCB only cares that 5 V arrives from J3
(some battery module) or from USB. Everything below follows "what flight
controllers do" unless stated.

- **RP2354B** (QFN-80, 2 MB in-package flash for code). Digital supplies
  (IOVDD, QSPI_IOVDD, USB_OTP_VDD, VREG_VIN) on V3V3_SYS; ADC_AVDD and
  VREG_AVDD on V3V3_ANA with 100 nF at each pin; DVDD from the internal core
  regulator with the datasheet inductor/capacitor. Decoupling per the
  RP2350 hardware design guide, one 100 nF per supply pin plus local bulk.
- **Clock/boot/reset**: 12 MHz crystal per the design guide; RUN held by
  PWR_GOOD (TPS62913 PG, open-drain, pulled up on V3V3_SYS) with a reset
  button to GND; BOOTSEL button; SWD on a small header. This is the whole
  supervisor: the MCU cannot run until the system rail is in regulation.
- **Sensors on V3V3_ANA over SPI**: BMI088 (accel + gyro CS, INT), BMP581
  (SPI), ADXL375 (SPI). Shared SCLK/MOSI/MISO, one CS each, interrupts to
  GPIO. Symbols/footprints from the repository libraries where present.
- **Logging on V3V3_SYS**: external QSPI NOR log flash sharing the QSPI bus
  with the in-package flash via a GPIO chip select if the datasheet allows
  it, otherwise on a plain SPI; **latched push-push microSD** wired for
  4-bit (PIO) with 10-47 uF + 100 nF at the socket.
- **Ports**: UART_GPS, UART_ELRS to two UART pin pairs; I2C_MAG to an I2C
  pair with 4.7 k pull-ups to V3V3_SYS.
- **Telemetry**: 5V_IN through a divider to an ADC pin; VBUS presence to a
  GPIO through a divider.
- **Actuators**: 8 PWM outputs on a 3-row 2.54 mm header (signal / SERVO_5V /
  GND). SERVO_5V is a pass-through of the J3 input *before* the ideal diode,
  so servo/ESC-signal-side current never crosses the board's rails or the
  LM66100; the 3 A BEC limit is the user's. Signals are 3.3 V logic (standard
  for servos and ESCs). Two status LEDs and a buzzer via a small MOSFET.
- Not included unless asked: pyro channels, CAN, external SPI header, OSD.

As-built GPIO map (the mcu sheet note is authoritative): GPIO0 FLASH_CS1
(QMI CS1); UART0 GPIO12 TX -> GPS_RX, GPIO13 RX <- GPS_TX; UART1 GPIO8 TX ->
ELRS_RX, GPIO9 RX <- ELRS_TX (port labels are named from the module's side);
I2C1 GPIO6 SDA / GPIO7 SCL (4.7k pull-ups); SPI0 GPIO18 SCK, GPIO19 MOSI,
GPIO16 MISO; CS GPIO20 accel, 21 gyro, 22 baro, 17 high-g; INT GPIO23/24/25/
29; microSD GPIO32 CLK, 33 CMD, 34-37 D0-D3, 38 DET; PWM1-8 on GPIO2,3,4,5,
10,11,14,15; LEDs GPIO26/27; buzzer GPIO28; ADC0 GPIO40 VIN_SENSE, ADC1
GPIO41 VBUS_SENSE; SWD on J10 (Cortex 10-pin order, pin 10 = RUN). Unused:
GPIO1, 30, 31, 39, 42-47 (no-connect).

Review record (board stage): BMI088 SDO1 (accel) and SDO2 (gyro) share
SENS_MISO with separate chip selects — datasheet 6.1: each SDO is driven only
while its own CSB is low; a reviewer's objection ("tie SDO2 to GND") applied
the I2C-address rule and was rejected. The local symbol MARV_Power:BMI088_SPI
only retypes SDO2 as passive for ERC. ADXL375 RESERVED pins tied per the
datasheet (pin 3 to VS or open -> VS; pin 11 to GND or open -> GND). The
sensors' VDDIO is V3V3_ANA while SPI is driven from V3V3_SYS; both are 3.3 V
nominal and the mismatch is within the digital-input abs-max margins.

Evidence-based details (RP2350 datasheet and hardware design guide, build
2026-08-20): all six QSPI pads are bonded out on the RP2354B and a second
QSPI device may share the bus with GPIO0/8/19/47 as QMI CS1 — the log flash
uses **GPIO0**. Internal core regulator: 3.3 uH on VREG_LX, 4.7 uF on DVDD,
VREG_FB tied to DVDD, VREG_AVDD through 33 Ohm + 4.7 uF. Crystal 12 MHz with
15 pF loads and 1 kOhm series on XOUT; USB 27 Ohm series; BOOTSEL = QSPI_SS
to GND through 1 kOhm. Decided where the documents are silent: RUN has no
debounce capacitor (button + PWR_GOOD only, as on Pico 2); 5V_IN and VBUS
sensing use 10k/15k dividers (5.25 V -> 3.15 V; 5.5 V -> 3.30 V = ADC_AVDD)
with 100 nF; unused GPIO left open. GPIO budget: 35 of 48 used, 13 spare, 6
ADC-capable pins spare.

## Open items and gates

- MCU sheet: RP2354B decoupling (per pin group, from the datasheet), ADC_AVDD
  and VREG_AVDD from V3V3_ANA with 100 nF each, VREG_VIN from V3V3_SYS, VBUS
  sense, reset/power-fail supervision (PWR_GOOD is the TPS62913 PG), 5V_IN
  voltage divider for telemetry, I2C pull-ups, UART TX/RX naming, QSPI flash,
  latched microSD with local bulk, USB series resistors.
- Servo count / signal voltage; actuator sheet.
- Exact MPNs; inductor saturation vs the TPS62913 current limit; ferrite bead
  DC resistance vs load regulation.
- Hardware: source insert/remove with logging active, max load, no-airflow
  soak, USB enumeration, rail noise measurements on V3V3_SYS and V3V3_ANA.
