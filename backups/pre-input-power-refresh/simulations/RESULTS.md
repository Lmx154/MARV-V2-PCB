## What the numbers mean

- P1a, WHY IT FAILS: Q1's gate net IS USB_VBUS, so VGS equals the forward drop across D1. With USB present D1 conducts, VGS = +0.613 V (positive means the P-channel is OFF) and the BEC can only reach V5_SYS through Q1's BODY DIODE. The 1.5 A splits D1 1.080 A / Q1 body diode 0.420 A at V5_SYS = 4.278 V. The predicate assumed the FET path wins when both sources are at 5.00 V; as wired, USB wins and the FET is held off. This is the schematic's stated intent ("USB present turns Q1 OFF, so a host can never back-feed the BEC"), so the predicate, not the board, is what disagrees with the netlist -- but the consequences below are real either way.
- P1a consequence, HAND ARITHMETIC from the simulated currents, not a thermal simulation: Q1 body diode dissipates 0.420 A x 0.701 V = 0.29 W (SOT-23, 100 C/W steady-state -> 29 C rise) and D1 dissipates 1.080 A x 0.613 V = 0.66 W (SOD-323, 80 C/W -> 53 C rise) while carrying 108% of its 1.0 A average forward rating. The 1.5 A case is a stress load, not the ~0.5 A the design budget puts on V5_SYS, but the split is the same shape at any current.
- P1b, WHY IT FAILS BY 59 mV: reaching 4.50 V at 0.5 A behind the assumed 0.10 ohm of connector resistance needs D1 to drop no more than 0.450 V, and the deck uses the datasheet MAXIMUM forward model, which gives 0.509 V at 0.5 A. A typical 1N5819WS passes; a worst-case one does not. This predicate is decided by the D1 forward-voltage spread, not by the OR topology -- the datasheet gives no typical VF number, only a log-log curve, so no typical run is offered here.
- P1b sensitivity: with RUSB = 0.35 ohm (the cable resistance the retired decks assumed) instead of the 0.10 ohm connector-only value, V5_SYS = 4.315 V.
- P1c detail: USB_VBUS floats to 0.015 V, VGS = -4.938 V (Q1 channel fully on), V5_SYS = 4.953 V with the channel carrying 0.500 A.
- P1c sensitivity (25 C datasheet max D1 reverse leakage, 0.5 mA at the 40 V rated VR, applied here at only 5 V of reverse bias and therefore conservative): back-feed 442 uA -- VIOLATES the 50 uA predicate -- USB_VBUS floats to 0.580 V, VGS -4.372 V (Q1 still fully on), V5_SYS 4.951 V. VBUS_SENSE, the R29 10k / R30 15k divider the RP2350 reads on GPIO to detect a cable, sits at 0.21 V with no cable attached; check that against the RP2350 GPIO input thresholds, which are NOT looked up in this deck. The USB LED D21 draws 0.00 mA in this state (0 mA means dark).
- P1c sensitivity (100 C datasheet max D1 reverse leakage, 10 mA at the 40 V rated VR, applied here at only 5 V of reverse bias and therefore conservative): back-feed 1660 uA -- VIOLATES the 50 uA predicate -- USB_VBUS floats to 3.881 V, VGS -0.828 V (Q1 NEAR ITS -0.5/-0.9/-1.3 V THRESHOLD BAND), V5_SYS 4.708 V. VBUS_SENSE, the R29 10k / R30 15k divider the RP2350 reads on GPIO to detect a cable, sits at 1.77 V with no cable attached; check that against the RP2350 GPIO input thresholds, which are NOT looked up in this deck. The USB LED D21 draws 1.01 mA in this state (0 mA means dark).
- P1d, WHY IT FAILS: before the step V5_SYS = 4.905 V with Q1's CHANNEL carrying the full 1.000 A (VGS = -4.905 V). Plugging USB in forward-biases D1, which drives VGS to +0.461 V and turns the channel off; V5_SYS then drops to 4.245 V and STAYS there, held up by Q1's body diode (0.708 A) in parallel with D1 (0.292 A) from a USB source behind 1 ohm. That is a 661 mV permanent step, not a transient, and the body diode then dissipates 0.708 A x 0.720 V = 0.51 W (51 C rise at 100 C/W). Hot-plugging USB while the aircraft is powered is the case this models.
- DOWNSTREAM CONSEQUENCE of the three V5_SYS failures: the lowest V5_SYS across every OR case run here is 4.245 V. The AP63203 needs VIN >= 3.8 V (UVLO 3.50 V rising typ, DS41326), and U12 needs 3.30 V + 145 mV of dropout at worst, so the 3.3 V rails keep running in all of them. What the failures cost is headroom, the 5 V pad rows on J7 (which are fed from 5V_IN, not V5_SYS, so they are not affected) and the dissipation quoted above -- not a loss of the avionics rail.
- P2b detail: V3V3_SYS reaches 99% of setpoint at 3.96 ms, consistent with the datasheet tSS = 4 ms soft-start; settled value 3.3000 V.
- NC2 as the brief specified it (C10/C11 cut to 2 x 4.7 uF) does NOT violate the undershoot limit: the run above gives V3V3_SYS min = 3.0779 V against the 3.00 V limit, whether the 4.7 uF part is taken at an assumed 25% DC-bias loss (3.5 uF each) or at the same 40% the 22 uF parts get. The undershoot limit is first violated at about 2 x 2.35 uF EFFECTIVE (2.9969 V), i.e. roughly 2 x 3.9 uF nominal at 40% derating. The registered negative control is therefore 1 uF effective each, which fails both the undershoot and the overshoot limits. Note that reducing COUT while the AP63203's internal compensation stays fixed pushes the modelled crossover up in proportion (60 kHz x 26.4/COUT), so at 2 x 1 uF the model is running at a crossover near fSW/3 where a real peak-current-mode converter would be subharmonically unstable -- something an averaged model cannot show, so the negative control understates how bad that case is.
- P3d detail, REPORTED WITHOUT A PREDICATE because the brief did not set one: V3V3_ANA reaches 90% of 3.3 V at 1.969 ms while V3V3_SYS does not until 3.900 ms, so V3V3_ANA sits up to 1.620 V ABOVE V3V3_SYS for about 1.93 ms. U12 is fed from V5_SYS, not from V3V3_SYS, so nothing holds the analog rail below the system rail once EN releases at 0.9 V on a still-rising V3V3_SYS.
- THE TASK BRIEF DESCRIBED U12 AS FED FROM V3V3_SYS; THE NETLIST PUTS IT ON V5_SYS (U12 pin 1 = IN -> V5_SYS, pin 3 = EN -> V3V3_SYS; DESIGN_SPEC.md agrees). The board wiring is the default run. The "fed from 3.3 V" sensitivity row shows why that matters: with 3.30 V in, the LDO is IN DROPOUT and stops regulating -- V3V3_ANA settles at 3.2748 V at 50 mA, exactly VIN minus the datasheet dropout resistance (22.6 mV at 50 mA), and the +/-10 mV settling measure never terminates (499 us of a 500 us window). Read the 25.17 mV "deviation" and the 13.33 mV "ringing" in that row as ARTEFACTS of an output that is a resistor, not as transient behaviour: the row is evidence that the V5_SYS feed on the board is the correct one, not evidence about the loop.
- NC3 as the brief specified it (C26 = 0.47 uF) does NOT fail: deviation 10.37 mV against the 60 mV limit, settling 0.96 us against 100 us, no ringing. Two separate reasons, and both are findings rather than excuses. (1) V3V3_ANA carries 1.6 uF of sensor decoupling (six 100 nF plus C59 1 uF) besides C26, so moving C26 from 2.2 uF to 0.47 uF only moves the node from 3.8 uF to 2.07 uF. (2) More fundamentally, in this behavioural LDO the load-step deviation is COUT-INDEPENDENT by construction: crossover scales as 1/COUT, so the product f_c x COUT -- which is what sets the deviation -- is constant, and the Routh determinant ratio is likewise independent of COUT. Sweeping C26 down to 10 nF with the downstream caps removed changes the deviation by under 2 mV and never produces ringing. THIS DECK THEREFORE CANNOT TEST THE DATASHEET 1 uF MINIMUM COUT, and the C26 = 1.1 uF derated PASS must not be read as evidence of stability at that capacitance. The registered negative control is instead a realistic wiring defect the predicates CAN see: U12 EN tied to V5_SYS rather than V3V3_SYS.

## What none of this proves

- **The AP63203 control loop.** The buck model is AVERAGED. It has no switching ripple, no subharmonic-oscillation check (D = 0.66 > 0.5, so slope compensation matters and is not modelled), no PSM / pulse-skipping light-load mode, no FSS jitter, no bootstrap refresh, no minimum on time, no hiccup and no thermal shutdown. Its crossover FC is an assumption, not a datasheet number; the sensitivity table shows what halving and doubling it does.
- **The TPS7A20 control loop.** Same class of caveat, and worse: the loop bandwidth is a reading off a printed transient figure. Worse still, the behavioural LDO has a COUT-INDEPENDENT phase margin by construction (the fixed compensation zero and the crossover both scale with 1/COUT, and the Routh determinant ratio comes out independent of COUT), so **this deck cannot test the datasheet's 1 uF minimum output capacitance**. A PASS on the C26 = 1.1 uF derated run is NOT evidence that the rail is stable at that capacitance; it only shows the deviation and settling predicates are insensitive to C26, which is itself the finding.
- **Layout.** Everything is lumped. No trace inductance, no ground impedance, no separation between the LDO pin and the sensor cluster several centimetres away, no switch-node loop area. The datasheet instruction to place C26 at the OUT pin cannot be checked here.
- **EMI, thermal and mechanical.** No conducted or radiated emission, no junction temperatures, no inductor saturation or temperature rise, no connector or solder-joint behaviour. The P1a / P1d body-diode dissipation numbers quoted above are hand arithmetic from the simulated currents, not a thermal simulation.
- **The BEC and the USB host.** Both are ideal voltage sources behind a lumped resistance. No BEC control loop, no current limit, no USB enumeration, negotiation, inrush or suspend behaviour.
- **Silicon tolerance spread.** Only the D1 forward drop is taken at its datasheet maximum. Q1 uses the vendor typical model; the AP63203 and TPS7A20 use typical datasheet values. No Monte Carlo, no temperature corners except the two explicit D1 reverse-leakage rows.
- **Anything measured on hardware.** Nothing here has been on a bench.
