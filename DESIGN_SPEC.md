# MARV V2 design specification

## Purpose and scope

Flight controller for rockets and drones, built to **stack on a MicoAir AM32 4-in-1 ESC** (2-6S, no BEC). The raw pack arrives on the ESC's FC-connection pad row and an on-board **AP63205WU buck (U26)** makes the 5 V rail; USB is the fallback source. MCU **RP2354B** (QFN-80, 2 MB in-package flash). Onboard sensors: ICM-45686 (6-axis IMU), BMP581, ADXL375. Logging: a QSPI socket that takes either a W25Q64 NOR flash or an APS6404L PSRAM, plus a latched (push-push) microSD socket. From the ESC the board also reads analog current sense and one-wire KISS telemetry. External daughterboards on solder-pad ports: GPS (UART), ELRS radio (UART), magnetometer (I2C) — they carry their own circuitry. Design rule: the simplest form of what the user states; for unspecified details, what flight controllers in the wild do.

The schematic implements power, ports, MCU, sensors, logging and PWM outputs: 8 sheets, 141 components, 105 nets, generated by `tools/build_power.py` (idempotent — it overwrites generated sheets, so edit the generator, not the sheets). Firmware and PCB layout are not done. Not for manufacture or flight.

U7 TPS62913RPUR (VQFN-HR-10 2x2 mm), 2.2 MHz spread-spectrum (S-CONF 6.04k), L2 2.2 uH (XGL4030-222, 7 A), 3x22 uF first-stage output, bead BLE18PS080SN1 (8.5 Ohm, 4 mOhm) + 2x22 uF after it, FB sensed after the bead, divider 15.8k/4.99k (3.33 V nominal, 3.30-3.38 V over VFB tolerance), NR/SS 470 nF, 2.2 nF VIN bypass, PG pulled up to V3V3_SYS. U12 TPS7A2033PDBVR (fixed 3.3 V, active discharge), 1 uF in / 1 uF out, EN driven by PWR_GOOD (the TPS62913 PG signal) so V3V3_ANA rises only after V3V3_SYS is up. Passive MPNs beyond the inductor and bead remain open (see Open items).

U26 AP63205WU-7 (VBAT -> 5V_IN), Diodes datasheet DS41326 Rev. 2-2. Values are Table 3 "Recommended Component Selections for AP63205" and Figure 21 verbatim: L3 4.7 uH, C73 10 uF input, C76/C77/C80 3 x 22 uF output (datasheet minimum is 2 x 22 uF), C75 100 nF bootstrap BST-SW. FB (pin 1) is a *sense* input on the fixed-output parts and connects straight to the 5 V output — only the adjustable AP63200/AP63201 take a divider. EN (pin 2) is tied to VBAT through R52 100k; the datasheet allows a direct connection (EN abs max 35 V), and the 100k is series protection plus a place to add the Eq.1/Eq.2 UVLO divider or the Eq.3 start-delay cap later. L3 is a Coilcraft **XGL4030-472MEC**: 4.7 uH ±20%, DCR 31.5 mOhm max, Isat 3.2 A at 20% / 4.4 A at 30% drop, Irms 4.8 A at 20 °C rise, 4.0 x 4.0 x 3.1 mm (Coilcraft Doc 1575-1). Isat(20%) clears the AP63205's high-side peak current limit at its 3.1 A maximum, so the inductor does not saturate even in a current-limit or hiccup event, and it reuses the XGL4030 footprint and 3D model already vendored for L2. Ripple at 25.2 V in / 2 A out is 0.78 A pk-pk, peak 2.39 A. **Open:** C73 at 10 uF/50 V input bias and all three C76/C77/C80 at 22 uF/25 V output bias each lose roughly half to DC-bias derating; C73's derated value remains open pending actual footprint measurements.

Modelled conversion loss (simulations/README.md): buck ~0.09 W at 300 mA, ~0.14 W at 500 mA (92% assumed efficiency), LDO ~0.02 W — these are the *V5_SYS -> 3.3 V* stages only; U26's own loss is not modelled. Treat 500 mA as a burst ceiling until the real enclosure is measured.

Source mux (U25 TPS2121, IN1 = 5V_IN priority, IN2 = USB_VBUS, reverse-current blocking on both inputs at all times): PR1 divider R42 32.4k/R43 10k selects IN1 above ~4.5 V rising (typ), releases it below ~4.4 V falling, with USB taking over V5_SYS within the ~100 us switchover time (CP2 grounded: internal-reference priority mode). OV1 divider R44 45.3k/R45 10k rejects IN1 above ~5.86 V typ (5.59-6.08 V over tolerance) — but with the on-board buck IN1 is a regulated 5.00 V ±1% node rather than an external BEC of unknown quality, so PR1 never releases in normal operation and OV1 can only trip on a U26 failure. It is now a backstop against a shorted high-side FET pushing VBAT onto 5V_IN, not the mis-plug guard it used to be; the servo row J13, wired upstream of U25, is exposed to that same failure. ILM R46 80.6k sets a ~1.5 A OUT current limit (OCP trips at 2.4x). SS C70 100 nF gives a ~6 ms soft-start ramp. ST (open-drain, HIGH = IN1 selected) drives PWR_SRC_ST -> GPIO39, pulled up by R47 10k to V3V3_SYS. C71/C72 100 nF bypass IN1/IN2.

### Battery monitoring

VBAT_SENSE (ADC0 / GPIO40, R27/R28 100k/10k, 36.3 V full scale, ~8.9 mV/LSB at 12 bit — a one-point calibration against a bench meter gets per-cell voltage well under 0.1 V error) plus the ESC's CURR output (ADC2 / GPIO42, 12.75 mV/A through R53/C78, ~0.063 A/LSB, integrated in firmware for mAh consumed) **are** the battery monitor. An INA226 (or any shunt-based coulomb counter) was rejected: it needs a shunt resistor in the pack's B+ or B- line, and this board deliberately has no such pass-through — the pack goes straight from the ESC's own VBAT pad, through heavy copper, to U26, with no series element to add drop or a fault point on a 2-6S propulsion line. Low-battery threshold is a firmware decision (per-cell voltage, since cell count is not sensed), not a board one.

## Physical design

Placement and routing are not done; this section is the brief that placement has to satisfy.

### Envelope and stack-up

Square board, **50 x 50 mm** (delivered by `tools/setup_pcb.py`, default `--size 50`): the summed F.CrtYd courtyard area is 1586 mm2 (with the 0201/0402 passives below; it was 1750 mm2 with 0402/0603 and 2055 mm2 with 0805 caps), i.e. **63.5 % fill at 50 mm**. 48 mm **still** fails the placement self-check even with the smaller passives — the packer finds no room for U24 and C19, they overlap each other, and SW1/J10 overlap on the top edge (68.9 % fill) — so 50 mm remains the smallest square that satisfies every placement rule single-sided. What binds at 48 mm is not passive area but the fixed blocks: four Ø8 mm grommet keepouts, the microSD socket, the USB-C receptacle and the two 2.54 mm through-hole blocks. Shrinking further needs double-sided assembly or dropping those through-hole blocks. Passives are **0201 (every resistor) / 0402 (ceramics <= 4.7 uF at <= 16 V, and the 50 V 100 nF and 15 pF parts) / 0603 (10-22 uF at 10 V) / 0805 (22 uF at 25 V, 10 uF at 50 V, 47 uF at 6.3 V)**; the polymer bulk cap keeps its EIA-7343 body. **0201 is not hand-reworkable** — nor is a 22 uF 0603 — and that is the deliberate trade for this envelope: JLC places both in assembly, a bench iron does not rework them, so a mis-stuffed board is a re-order, not a repair. **4-layer SIG-GND-PWR-SIG, 1.6 mm**, JLC-standard stack-up. Maximum component height **4.4 mm above the top surface** — the tallest parts today are L2 and L3 at 3.1 mm and the USB-C receptacle at ~4.2 mm (check against the real drawing). **Single-sided assembly: every component is on F.Cu.** B.Cu carries routing and pads only, so the board can be reflowed once and sits flat on the ESC below it. Weight is not constrained.

### Mounting and orientation

Standard **30.5 x 30.5 mm** four-hole pattern, centred on the board. Each hole is **Ø4.0 mm NPTH** for a Ø3 mm silicone grommet, with a **Ø5.0 mm copper-free annulus** and a **Ø8.0 mm top-side courtyard keepout** for the grommet flange (footprint `MARV_Packages:MountingHole_4.0mm_Grommet`, symbols H1-H4 on the mcu sheet). Board orientation: the **front edge is the bottom edge (-Y)** and it faces the ESC's pad row when the two boards are stacked in the same orientation.

### Connection philosophy

Carried over from MARV V1.2 and madflight practice, revised for the IO-array board: **solder pads only for the ESC row (J3) and the DBG landing (J10)** — the two ports that mate with something fixed and pad-shaped (the ESC's own pads, a debug probe). **Every other external signal — GPS, ELRS, magnetometer and every spare GPIO — leaves on the 2.54 mm J6 IO array**, madflight style: each row pairs a signal pin (outer column, board-edge side) with a power-or-GND pin (inner column) directly next to it, so a 3-wire module lead (signal, power, ground) plugs straight across two adjacent rows of the one block instead of needing its own connector. The PWM5-8 servo block (J12-J14) keeps its own 2.54 mm through-hole rows for the same reason — servo leads are 3-wire and expect 2.54 mm centres. No JST-GH, no pin headers for buses. The two solder-pad connectors (J3, J10) share one footprint family — SMD pads **1.4 x 2.2 mm oval on 2.00 mm pitch**, F.Cu/F.Mask only, a silkscreen label per pad added at layout, and a pin-1 mark. **Row order must match the mating device**, which is why J3 is defined by the ESC silkscreen rather than by any convention of ours.

## Decisions

- **Pack-powered from the ESC, not 5 V-in**: the board stacks on a MicoAir AM32 4-in-1 ESC that has no BEC, so it takes raw VBAT (2-6S) off the ESC's own pad row and makes its own 5 V with U26 (AP63205WU). This replaces the earlier 5 V-in-only architecture and its XT30 input (J3 XT30PW-M, D23 SMAJ5.0A, C16/C67/C68/C69 all removed). Consequences: a 6-25.2 V node now exists on the board and is confined to J3 pin 7, C73/C74, R52, R27 and U26 pin 3; there is no TVS on it; and the servo 5 V bus is the buck output, so the buck's 2 A is shared between avionics and PWM5-8.
- **AP63205WU (fixed 5 V) rather than an adjustable buck**: fixed output means no feedback divider to get wrong and no divider noise path, 32 V absolute maximum covers 6S with margin, 1.1 MHz keeps the 4.7 uH inductor at 4 x 4 x 3.1 mm, and the ±6% spread spectrum plus the part's ringing-free SW driver matter on a board carrying a magnetometer port and three MEMS sensors. Its 2 A is the binding limit on total board + servo current.
- **PWM1-4 to the ESC pads, PWM5-8 to a servo block**: the four motor signals go straight to the stacked ESC on solder pads, so no motor signal leaves the stack on a connector; the remaining four PWM channels keep a conventional 3x4 servo header for gimbals/airbrakes/deployment.
- **WS2812C-2020 instead of two discrete LEDs**: one 2 x 2 mm addressable pixel replaces D20/D21 + R31/R32, frees GPIO27, and its 2.7 V DIN input threshold works from 3.3 V CMOS (a 5050 WS2812B needs 0.7 x VDD = 3.5 V and would not). Costs one timing-critical GPIO instead of two plain ones.
- **U25 TPS2121 priority mux over dual LM66100 ideal diodes**: LM66100's recommended-operating max of 5.5 V and absolute max of 6 V sit too close to real BEC outputs, and it offered no over-voltage lockout, no current limit, and up to 1 A of reverse-activation current; TPS2121 (22 V abs-max) gives all three — OV lockout, ILM current limit, reverse-current blocking — in one part.
- **Split V3V3_SYS (buck) / V3V3_ANA (LDO)**: flash and microSD are supply-transient sources so they stay on the 3 A buck with generous local bulk; the LDO carries only the MEMS sensors and RP2354B ADC_AVDD (~10-20 mA), so its ~20-35 mW dissipation at 1.7 V drop is free and its noise floor beats a generic buck's.
- **ICM-45686 replaces the BMI088**: a single-chip-select 6-axis IMU (one AP_CS, one AP_SDO on SENS_MISO) simplifies the sensor bus to one pull-up and one interrupt pair per part instead of BMI088's separate accelerometer/gyroscope CSB and SDO lines; see LIBRARIES.md for the datasheet-derived pin map.
- **GPIO0 as second QSPI chip select (QMI CS1) for the log flash**: all six QSPI pads are bonded out on RP2354B and GPIO0/8/19/47 can serve as QMI CS1 per Raspberry Pi "Hardware design with RP2350" SS3.1-3.2; QSPI_SS is reserved for BOOTSEL and cannot be reused as a second CS.

Review record (defects found and fixed in the schematic review):
- LM66100 rating (5.5 V rec-op / 6 V abs-max) too close to real BEC outputs -> replaced with TPS2121.
- 5V_IN bulk capacitance below the regulator's datasheet minimum, no TVS -> added C67/C68/C69 and D23 SMAJ5.0A.
- J6/J7 JST-GH pin order didn't match Pixhawk DS-009 -> corrected to 1 VCC, 2 TX, 3 RX, 4 GND.
- VIN_SENSE divider saturated the ADC at 5.5 V input -> widened to R27/R28 22k/10k.
- LDO (U12) had no enable sequencing -> EN now driven by PWR_GOOD, after V3V3_SYS.
- RP2354B VREG_AVDD was split onto V3V3_ANA while VREG_VIN needs to power up with it -> moved to V3V3_SYS.
- Sensor SPI chip-select lines were floating -> added R48-R51 10k pull-ups to V3V3_ANA.
- ERC's single global label check was disabled -> re-enabled (0/0 with it on).

Changes in the ESC-stack revision:
- 5 V input (J3 XT30PW-M, D23 TVS, C16/C67/C68/C69) replaced by the ESC pad row plus U26 AP63205WU.
- VIN_SENSE (22k/10k off 5V_IN) became VBAT_SENSE (100k/10k off VBAT, 36.3 V full scale).
- J6/J7/J9 JST-GH connectors and the J10 2x05 SWD header became solder pad rows; BZ1 became the J15 pads.
- D20/D21/R31/R32 became a single WS2812C-2020 (D20) with R55 and C79.
- U24 128 Mbit flash became a 64 Mbit / PSRAM socket in a 150-mil SOIC-8 land.
- The nine previously no-connect GPIOs were brought out on J16.

Changes in the small-passives revision:
- **Buzzer deleted entirely**: J15 pads, Q20, D22, R33, R34 and the BUZZ_* nets are gone. GPIO28 is no longer a buzzer output; it goes to J16 pin 12 (net IO_GPIO28, silk "IO28"), which was the block's second ground. A buzzer is now an off-board integration choice on any spare IO, not a board feature. 139 -> 134 components, 107 -> 105 nets.
- **Smallest passives the ratings allow**: every resistor is 0201, ceramics <= 4.7 uF are 0402, the 10-22 uF 10 V ceramics are 0603 (C10-C12 / C23/C24 22 uF became X5R 0603 GRM188R61A226ME15), the 25 V and 50 V bulk caps are 0805 and C66 47 uF is 0805. Courtyard sum 1750 -> 1586 mm2. The 22 uF 0603 parts lose ~40 % to DC-bias at 3.3 V (the ngspice decks still carry the nominal 66 uF — see Open items).
- **USB net-class clearance 0.20 -> 0.18 mm**: an R_0201_0603Metric land has 0.18 mm between its own two pads, which on R22/R23 are two different USB nets, so 0.20 mm made the package itself a DRC error. The pair still routes at its 0.20 mm coupled gap and 0.18 mm is far above the 0.127 mm board/fab minimum.

## Heat and interference

- Enclosed, no forced airflow; qualification ambient 60 C; junctions below 100 C; exposed-pad vias and ground copper are the heat path.
- 4-layer SIG-GND-PWR-SIG: solid L2 ground; L3 pours for VBAT/5V_IN/V5_SYS/V3V3_SYS with ground fill; **U26 and L3 at the bottom-left corner next to the VBAT pad**, its input loop (C73/C74 to VIN/GND) as small as possible and the SW node small and quiet; U7 and its inductor between U26 and the MCU, SW loop over L2 ground, shielded inductor, FB sense after the bead routed away from SW; U12 and the sensors on the ground-referenced analog island near the centre, away from both switchers, the ESC row and any inductor; no inductor under the IMU/barometer; servo and ESC return currents off the sensor ground; magnetometer external, away from power leads and the ESC. The board sits directly above a 4-in-1 ESC switching motor currents, so the IMU cluster's ground reference and the VBAT/5V_IN loops are the two things that decide whether the sensors are usable.

## Open items and validation

- PCB layout not done: see **Physical design** for the envelope, stack-up, mounting pattern and edge allocation that placement has to satisfy. Final board size is set at placement (the smallest square that fits). The VBAT and 5V_IN copper must be sized for the buck's 2 A plus the servo draw, not just the avionics budget.
- Passive MPNs: L20 AOTA-B201610S3R3-101-T and L3 XGL4030-472MEC assigned; the 22 uF ceramics now name parts (GRM188R61A226ME15 at 10 V 0603, GRM21BR61E226ME44 at 25 V 0805, GRM21BR61H106KE43 for C73). The rest stay open. The buzzer and its driver are gone entirely — a buzzer is now a firmware-free integration choice on J6 GPIO28 (row 15), not a board feature.
- **The ngspice decks carry nominal capacitance, not derated.** `simulations/*.cir` set `COUT=66u` for the 3.3 V stage, but C10-C12 are now 22 uF / 10 V X5R **0603**, which lose ~40 % at 3.3 V DC bias (~40 uF effective first stage, ~26 uF after the bead). The modelled rail is optimistic by that margin; re-run the decks with the derated value before treating any transient result as a bound. The package change did not change the nominal value, only the bias sensitivity (an 0805 X7S lost ~20-25 %).
- **Buck output capacitance nominal 66 uF (C76/C77/C80 each 22 uF), derated to ~44 uF per AP63205 EVB guide.** C73 10 uF/50 V at 25 V input bias and all three 22 uF/25 V output caps at 5 V output bias each lose roughly half to DC-bias derating; C73's ~5 uF nominal remains open pending actual footprint measurements.
- **J3 pad pitch is unconfirmed.** MicoAir publishes no pad drawing for the AM32 4-in-1 FC row; 2.00 mm is assumed. The pad *order* is authoritative. Measure the physical ESC before fab.
- **WS2812C VDD.** Its absolute maximum supply is 5.3 V and V5_SYS can be USB VBUS, which is not guaranteed below that on a non-compliant charger (the TPS2121's OV2 is grounded, so the USB channel has no over-voltage cut-off). Acceptable risk or a reason to add OV2 — lead's call.
- **Optional series resistors on the PWM outputs, not yet added** (applies to PWM1-4 on the ESC pads as well as PWM5-8 on the servo block).
- Enclosure thermal measurement needed before sustained loads above 500 mA (see Heat and interference).
- Firmware: ICM-45686 (U21) defaults to I2C/I3C protocol interpretation on its AP interface pins after power-up (DS-000577 Rev 1.0 Sec 10.1); since AP_SCLK/AP_SDA (SENS_SCK/SENS_MOSI) are hardwired as pure SPI signals here, firmware must assert IMU_CS (AP_CS, pin 12) low for the first SPI transaction before relying on SPI0 responses (Table 10 pin 12 note: "Connect to VDDIO if using AP I3C/I2C interface").
- 3D models: every footprint the board uses now resolves inside the project (`MARV_Packages.pretty` + `MARV_Packages.3dshapes`; provenance, licences and SHA256 in `MARV_Packages.3dshapes/PROVENANCE.md`). Three bodies are stand-ins (ICM-45686, BMP581, ADXL375), the WS2812C lens step is scaled rather than dimensioned, and two TI package heights are assumed, so run the visual checks listed in LIBRARIES.md in the 3D viewer before fab — including the ADXL375 pin-1 corner. The pad rows, mounting holes and test points carry no 3D model by design; `tools/audit_footprints.py --no-3d-ok` (default `^(PadRow_|MountingHole_|TestPoint_)`) is what makes that a pass rather than a defect.
- MCU sheet: VBUS sense and reset/power-fail supervision (PWR_GOOD is the TPS62913 PG) still to review; servo count / signal voltage / actuator sheet; inductor saturation vs the TPS62913 current limit; ferrite bead DC resistance vs load regulation.
- Hardware: source insert/remove with logging active, max load, no-airflow soak, USB enumeration, rail noise measurements on V3V3_SYS and V3V3_ANA.
- Checks: `kicad-cli sch erc` (0/0, single global label check enabled), netlist export to `reports/power-netlist.xml` + `tools/check_power_netlist.py <netlist>` (231 critical pin mappings, plus the VBAT -> U26 -> 5V_IN -> U25 -> V5_SYS boundary, the ESC pad-row order, the J6 IO-array row map, the ten sensor test points and the PWM1-4 / PWM5-8 split), `tools/run_power_checks.py` (behavioral ngspice; simulations/README.md carries a Status line; non-zero exit on any violated predicate — the decks start at the buck output and prove nothing about U26 itself), PDF in `reports/`, and `tools/audit_footprints.py <netlist>` (every component has a resolvable footprint file and 3D model). 141 components, 105 nets. The models are architectural: no loop stability, EMI, noise, USB compliance or enclosure temperature is proven by them.
