# MARV V2 design specification

## Purpose and scope

Flight controller for rockets and drones, built to **stack on a MicoAir AM32 4-in-1 ESC** (2-6S, no BEC). The raw pack arrives on the ESC's FC-connection pad row and an on-board **AP63205WU buck (U26)** makes the 5 V rail; USB is the fallback source. MCU **RP2354B** (QFN-80, 2 MB in-package flash). Onboard sensors: ICM-45686 (6-axis IMU), BMP581, ADXL375. Logging: a latched (push-push) microSD socket, plus an **optional, unpopulated** QSPI expansion socket on the back that takes either a W25Q64 NOR flash or an APS6404L PSRAM. Firmware and logs use the RP2354B's in-package 2 MB flash and the microSD; the socket is a user expansion, not a board feature. From the ESC the board also reads analog current sense and one-wire KISS telemetry. External daughterboards on solder-pad ports: GPS (UART), ELRS radio (UART), magnetometer (I2C) — they carry their own circuitry. Design rule: the simplest form of what the user states; for unspecified details, what flight controllers in the wild do.

The schematic implements power, ports, MCU, sensors, logging and PWM outputs: 7 sheets, 140 components, 105 nets, generated by `tools/build_power.py` (idempotent — it overwrites generated sheets, so edit the generator, not the sheets). Firmware and PCB layout are not done. Not for manufacture or flight.

U7 TPS62913RPUR (VQFN-HR-10 2x2 mm), 2.2 MHz spread-spectrum (S-CONF 6.04k), L2 2.2 uH (XGL4030-222, 7 A), 3x22 uF first-stage output, bead BLE18PS080SN1 (8.5 Ohm, 4 mOhm) + 2x22 uF after it, FB sensed after the bead, divider 15.8k/4.99k (3.33 V nominal, 3.30-3.38 V over VFB tolerance), NR/SS 470 nF, 2.2 nF VIN bypass, PG pulled up to V3V3_SYS. U12 TPS7A2033PDBVR (fixed 3.3 V, active discharge), 1 uF in / 1 uF out, EN driven by PWR_GOOD (the TPS62913 PG signal) so V3V3_ANA rises only after V3V3_SYS is up. **C19, the V5_SYS bulk, is 100 uF / 6.3 V polymer, ESR <= 40 mOhm** (Panasonic 6TPE100MAZB or a KEMET T520/T530 B case) in `Capacitor_Tantalum_SMD:CP_EIA-3528-21_Kemet-B` — down from 220 uF / 10 V in an EIA-7343-31 D case. It is the hold-up that carries the load across the TPS2121's 100 us switchover, so the reduction was made by re-running the decks, not by inspection: see "Open items" and simulations/README.md. Passive MPNs beyond the inductor and bead remain open (see Open items).

U26 AP63205WU-7 (VBAT -> 5V_IN), Diodes datasheet DS41326 Rev. 2-2. Values are Table 3 "Recommended Component Selections for AP63205" and Figure 21 verbatim: L3 4.7 uH, C73 10 uF input, C76/C77/C80 3 x 22 uF output (datasheet minimum is 2 x 22 uF), C75 100 nF bootstrap BST-SW. FB (pin 1) is a *sense* input on the fixed-output parts and connects straight to the 5 V output — only the adjustable AP63200/AP63201 take a divider. EN (pin 2) is tied to VBAT through R52 100k; the datasheet allows a direct connection (EN abs max 35 V), and the 100k is series protection plus a place to add the Eq.1/Eq.2 UVLO divider or the Eq.3 start-delay cap later. L3 is a Coilcraft **XGL4030-472MEC**: 4.7 uH ±20%, DCR 31.5 mOhm max, Isat 3.2 A at 20% / 4.4 A at 30% drop, Irms 4.8 A at 20 °C rise, 4.0 x 4.0 x 3.1 mm (Coilcraft Doc 1575-1). Isat(20%) clears the AP63205's high-side peak current limit at its 3.1 A maximum, so the inductor does not saturate even in a current-limit or hiccup event, and it reuses the XGL4030 footprint and 3D model already vendored for L2. Ripple at 25.2 V in / 2 A out is 0.78 A pk-pk, peak 2.39 A. **Open:** C73 at 10 uF/50 V input bias and all three C76/C77/C80 at 22 uF/25 V output bias each lose roughly half to DC-bias derating; C73's derated value remains open pending actual footprint measurements.

Modelled conversion loss (simulations/README.md): buck ~0.09 W at 300 mA, ~0.14 W at 500 mA (92% assumed efficiency), LDO ~0.02 W — these are the *V5_SYS -> 3.3 V* stages only; U26's own loss is not modelled. Treat 500 mA as a burst ceiling until the real enclosure is measured.

Source mux (U25 TPS2121, IN1 = 5V_IN priority, IN2 = USB_VBUS, reverse-current blocking on both inputs at all times): PR1 divider R42 32.4k/R43 10k selects IN1 above ~4.5 V rising (typ), releases it below ~4.4 V falling, with USB taking over V5_SYS within the ~100 us switchover time (CP2 grounded: internal-reference priority mode). OV1 divider R44 45.3k/R45 10k rejects IN1 above ~5.86 V typ (5.59-6.08 V over tolerance) — but with the on-board buck IN1 is a regulated 5.00 V ±1% node rather than an external BEC of unknown quality, so PR1 never releases in normal operation and OV1 can only trip on a U26 failure. It is now a backstop against a shorted high-side FET pushing VBAT onto 5V_IN, not the mis-plug guard it used to be; the servo rows (IO block J7 rows 7-10) now sit downstream of the mux on V5_SYS rather than tapping 5V_IN directly, so they get the same OV/current-limit protection as GPS/ELRS instead of being exposed to that failure. ILM R46 80.6k sets a ~1.5 A OUT current limit (OCP trips at 2.4x). SS C70 100 nF gives a ~6 ms soft-start ramp. ST (open-drain, HIGH = IN1 selected) drives PWR_SRC_ST -> GPIO39, pulled up by R47 10k to V3V3_SYS. C71/C72 100 nF bypass IN1/IN2.

### Battery monitoring

VBAT_SENSE (ADC0 / GPIO40, R27/R28 100k/10k, 36.3 V full scale, ~8.9 mV/LSB at 12 bit — a one-point calibration against a bench meter gets per-cell voltage well under 0.1 V error) plus the ESC's CURR output (ADC2 / GPIO42, 12.75 mV/A through R53/C78, ~0.063 A/LSB, integrated in firmware for mAh consumed) **are** the battery monitor. An INA226 (or any shunt-based coulomb counter) was rejected: it needs a shunt resistor in the pack's B+ or B- line, and this board deliberately has no such pass-through — the pack goes straight from the ESC's own VBAT pad, through heavy copper, to U26, with no series element to add drop or a fault point on a 2-6S propulsion line. Low-battery threshold is a firmware decision (per-cell voltage, since cell count is not sensed), not a board one.

## Physical design

Placement and routing are not done; this section is the brief that placement has to satisfy.

### Envelope and stack-up

Rectangular board, **46.6 x 41.2 mm** (delivered by `tools/setup_pcb.py`, defaults `BOARD_W`/`BOARD_H`; front courtyard fill 68.1 %, back 9.6 %; 40.0 mm and 39.0 mm heights were re-tried after this revision and still fail the packer — at 40.0 mm C21 (V5_SYS bulk) has no slot and C24 is 6.5 mm from U7, at 39.0 mm C20 and C21 both have none, C24 is 6.1 mm out and the IO block's J8/G end captions leave the board), starting from the madflight FC3v2 envelope (50.7 x 41.6 mm) and shrunk to the smallest size that still passes every placement rule. This revision moved the ESC pad row J3, the DBG pad row J10 and TP1-TP10 to the **back** (see "Back side" below), which gave back the 7 mm left-edge pocket J3 used to need, the bottom-right corner J10 used to need, and the ten 1 mm pads TP1-TP10 used to take out of the sensor island — the three things the previous 48.7 x 41.2 mm front-only envelope was standing on. At the new size, 46.6 mm is where the IO block's fixed row-caption band reaches the right-hand grommet keepouts, 39.0 mm is where the microSD socket on the top edge reaches the top mounting-hole pair (`python3 tools/setup_pcb.py --dry-run` checks placement rules against whatever size is actually built). The mounting square is **off-centre in X**: with nothing left on the front outboard of the left holes, the left margin is now just what the grommet keepout itself needs — the standard 30.5 mm pitch sits **4.0 mm in from the left edge** (Ø6.5 mm grommet-flange courtyard, 3.25 mm radius, plus 0.75 mm to the board edge) and (46.6 - 4.0 - 30.5 =) **12.1 mm in from the right edge** — the wider margin on the right is where the 3-column IO block sits, clear of the grommet keepouts. Holes are centred in Y. Passives are **0201 (every resistor) / 0402 (ceramics <= 4.7 uF at <= 16 V, and the 50 V 100 nF and 15 pF parts) / 0603 (10-22 uF at 10 V) / 0805 (22 uF at 25 V, 10 uF at 50 V, 47 uF at 6.3 V)**; the polymer bulk cap C19 is now an EIA-3528 (B) case, down from EIA-7343 (D). **0201 is not hand-reworkable** — nor is a 22 uF 0603 — and that is the deliberate trade for this envelope: JLC places both in assembly, a bench iron does not rework them, so a mis-stuffed board is a re-order, not a repair. **4-layer SIG-GND-PWR-SIG, 1.6 mm**, JLC-standard stack-up. Maximum component height **4.4 mm above the top surface** — the tallest parts today are L2 and L3 at 3.1 mm and the USB-C receptacle at ~4.2 mm (check against the real drawing). **Single-sided assembly: every component that is fitted is on F.Cu.** B.Cu carries routing, pads and unpopulated (DNP) lands only, so the board can be reflowed once and sits flat on the ESC below it — the bare-pad exception this permits is exactly what J3, J10 and TP1-TP10 use, and the DNP exception is exactly what the U24/C62/C63 expansion land uses; see "Back side" below. Weight is not constrained.

### Mounting and orientation

Standard **30.5 x 30.5 mm** four-hole pattern, off-centre in X (see "Envelope and stack-up"): the left holes sit **4.0 mm in from the left edge** — the Ø6.5 mm grommet-flange courtyard (3.25 mm radius) plus 0.75 mm to the board edge — and the right holes follow at the 30.5 mm pitch, 12.1 mm in from the right edge; both pairs are centred in Y. Each hole is **Ø4.0 mm NPTH** for a Ø3 mm silicone grommet, with a **Ø5.0 mm copper-free annulus** (all-layer, so it is also what the back-side bare pads — J3, J10, TP1-TP10, see "Back side" — have to clear, not just a front-side rule) and a **Ø6.5 mm top-side courtyard keepout** for the grommet flange (footprint `MARV_Packages:MountingHole_4.0mm_Grommet`, symbols H1-H4 on the mcu sheet). The keepout was Ø8.0 mm before this revision; madflight's own mounting pins sit only about 2.3 mm from the hole edge, so the extra clearance in an 8 mm courtyard was buying nothing the flange itself needs — it was costing ~68 mm2 of placeable area across the four holes, and shrinking it to Ø6.5 mm (matching the flange, not the flange plus a hand-clearance margin) is part of what makes the smaller envelope above fit. Board orientation: the **front edge is the bottom edge (-Y)** and it faces the ESC's pad row when the two boards are stacked in the same orientation.

### Connection philosophy

Carried over from MARV V1.2 and madflight practice, revised for the 3-column IO block: **solder pads only for the ESC row (J3) and the DBG landing (J10)** — the two ports that mate with something fixed and pad-shaped (the ESC's own pads, a debug probe). **Every other external signal — GPS, ELRS, magnetometer, PWM5-8 and every exposed spare GPIO — leaves on the 2.54 mm J6/J7/J8 IO block**, madflight FC3v2 style: each row is a signal pin (J6, innermost column, next to the MCU) with its own power pin (J7, middle column) and its own GND pin (J8, outermost column, at the board edge) directly beside it, so a 3-wire module lead or a servo plug goes straight across one row of the one block instead of needing its own connector. There is no separate servo header any more — PWM5-8 share this same block, on the same footing as GPS/ELRS/MAG. No JST-GH, no pin headers for buses. The two solder-pad connectors (J3, J10) share one footprint family — SMD pads **1.4 x 2.2 mm oval on 2.00 mm pitch**, authored on F.Cu/F.Mask and **placed flipped to the back (B.Cu/B.Mask)** by `tools/setup_pcb.py` — see "Back side" — with a silkscreen label per pad, mirrored text on B.SilkS, added at layout, and a pin-1 mark. **Row order must match the mating device**, which is why J3 is defined by the ESC silkscreen rather than by any convention of ours.

### Back side

`J3`, `J10`, TP1-TP10 and the optional expansion land `U24`/`C62`/`C63` are on **B.Cu**, not F.Cu. Assembly stays single-sided — **nothing on the back is ever reflowed**: the two solder-pad rows and the ten test points are bare pads, and U24/C62/C63 are **DNP** (`(dnp yes)` + `(in_bom no)` in the schematic, so they are not bought, not placed and not in `power_bom.csv` as fitted parts). All of them are authored like every other footprint on F.Cu and then placed **flipped** by `tools/setup_pcb.py`, which mirrors pads, mask, silkscreen and courtyard onto the matching B.* layers (KiCad's own layer bookkeeping on flip — see LIBRARIES.md's pad-row and test-point notes). This is exactly the exception `"B.Cu carries routing, pads and unpopulated lands only"` (see "Envelope and stack-up") already allows; the board is still reflowed once and still sits flat on the ESC below it. `tools/setup_pcb.py`'s `check_back()` asserts both halves of that rule — exactly which refs are flipped, **and** that every flipped part which is not a pad row or a test point really does carry the DNP attribute — so a socket that silently became a populated back-side component fails the build.

**Why these move to the back:** the board stacks directly on top of the ESC, back side down, so J3 facing the ESC lets the 8-wire harness run straight down between two fixed, order-matched pad rows instead of around an edge; J10 and TP1-TP10 are bench-only connections, read with the stack pulled apart, so their reachability does not depend on which side of the board they are on. Moving those three off the front is what let the envelope shrink from 48.7 x 41.2 mm to 46.6 x 41.2 mm (see "Envelope and stack-up"): it freed the 7 mm left-edge pocket J3 used to need, the bottom-right corner J10 used to need, and the ten 1 mm pads TP1-TP10 used to take out of the sensor island. The QSPI socket followed for a different reason — it is an **option**, not a feature, and an unpopulated land costs nothing on the back while the 7.5 x 5.5 mm it used to occupy under the MCU on the front is what the MCU decoupling and the U7 output caps got back. The cost is on silkscreen: the front loses every board-level silk label except the IO block's, which now sit in a fixed reserved band (see "IO block (J6/J7/J8)"); every back-side reference designator and pad label is mirrored text on B.SilkS instead, readable when the board is viewed from the back.

**The QSPI expansion land (U24, C62, C63).** A 150-mil SOIC-8 land against the MCU's QSPI pads 70-75, which face down because U20 is at 180°, so the six QSPI nets drop straight through the board and every stub is one via plus the 1.6 mm board thickness. Beside it, mirrored on B.SilkS, is the legend that makes an unpopulated land usable: **`FLASH / PSRAM EXPANSION` / `SOIC-8 150mil, QMI CS1` / `W25Q64JVSSIQ or` / `APS6404L-3SQR-SN`**, plus an explicit `1` pin-1 marker beside pin 1 (the part number line is split over two lines because one line of both MPNs is 18.2 mm at the 0.6 mm DRC text minimum and the clear corridor on the back is 18 mm). **R35, the FLASH_CS1 10k pull-up, is NOT DNP** and stays on the front: GPIO0 has to be held deselected whether or not the socket is fitted. The nets are unchanged — `tools/check_power_netlist.py` still checks all eight U24 pins against QSPI_SCLK/SD0-SD3/FLASH_CS1/V3V3_SYS/GND — and it additionally asserts that U24/C62/C63 carry `dnp` + `exclude_from_bom` in the netlist, that nothing else on the board does, and that R35 does not.

### ESC connection (J3)

`J3`, `MARV_Packages:PadRow_1x08_P2.00mm`, ESC-datasheet style — pad order and function are the MicoAir AM32 4-in-1's own FC-connection row, read left to right off its silkscreen. **The pads are on the back (B.Cu), vertical, CURR at the top, hard against the LEFT board edge** — pad outer edge 0.5 mm in from Edge.Cuts (0.2 mm above the 0.3 mm copper-to-edge rule), exactly as the DBG row J10 sits against the bottom edge, and centred in Y between the two left grommets. The eight pads on 2.00 mm pitch span y = ±7.0 mm of centres (±7.7 mm of copper), and the grommets' Ø5.0 mm copper keepout at y = ±15.25 mm reaches only |y| = 12.75 mm, so the row clears it by 5.05 mm with nothing but that keepout deciding the length. Pad 7 (VBAT) lands at y = -5, about 5 mm and one via from U26's own input. Labels are inboard (+x) on B.SilkS with the `ESC` legend above the row; see "Back side". ## Decisions

- **Pack-powered from the ESC, not 5 V-in**: the board stacks on a MicoAir AM32 4-in-1 ESC that has no BEC, so it takes raw VBAT (2-6S) off the ESC's own pad row and makes its own 5 V with U26 (AP63205WU). This replaces the earlier 5 V-in-only architecture and its XT30 input (J3 XT30PW-M, D23 SMAJ5.0A, C16/C67/C68/C69 all removed). Consequences: a 6-25.2 V node now exists on the board and is confined to J3 pin 7, C73/C74, R52, R27 and U26 pin 3; there is no TVS on it; and the servo 5 V bus is V5_SYS, downstream of the U25 mux, so both the buck's 2 A and the mux's ~1.5 A limit are shared between avionics and PWM5-8.
- **AP63205WU (fixed 5 V) rather than an adjustable buck**: fixed output means no feedback divider to get wrong and no divider noise path, 32 V absolute maximum covers 6S with margin, 1.1 MHz keeps the 4.7 uH inductor at 4 x 4 x 3.1 mm, and the ±6% spread spectrum plus the part's ringing-free SW driver matter on a board carrying a magnetometer port and three MEMS sensors. Its 2 A is the binding limit on total board + servo current.
- **PWM1-4 to the ESC pads, PWM5-8 to the IO block**: the four motor signals go straight to the stacked ESC on solder pads, so no motor signal leaves the stack on a connector; the remaining four PWM channels for gimbals/airbrakes/deployment share the IO block (rows 7-10) with every other external signal instead of a dedicated servo header.
- **WS2812C-2020 instead of two discrete LEDs**: one 2 x 2 mm addressable pixel replaces D20/D21 + R31/R32, frees GPIO27, and its 2.7 V DIN input threshold works from 3.3 V CMOS (a 5050 WS2812B needs 0.7 x VDD = 3.5 V and would not). Costs one timing-critical GPIO instead of two plain ones.
- **U25 TPS2121 priority mux over dual LM66100 ideal diodes**: LM66100's recommended-operating max of 5.5 V and absolute max of 6 V sit too close to real BEC outputs, and it offered no over-voltage lockout, no current limit, and up to 1 A of reverse-activation current; TPS2121 (22 V abs-max) gives all three — OV lockout, ILM current limit, reverse-current blocking — in one part.
- **Split V3V3_SYS (buck) / V3V3_ANA (LDO)**: flash and microSD are supply-transient sources so they stay on the 3 A buck with generous local bulk; the LDO carries only the MEMS sensors and RP2354B ADC_AVDD (~10-20 mA), so its ~20-35 mW dissipation at 1.7 V drop is free and its noise floor beats a generic buck's.
- **ICM-45686 replaces the BMI088**: a single-chip-select 6-axis IMU (one AP_CS, one AP_SDO on SENS_MISO) simplifies the sensor bus to one pull-up and one interrupt pair per part instead of BMI088's separate accelerometer/gyroscope CSB and SDO lines; see LIBRARIES.md for the datasheet-derived pin map.
- **GPIO0 as second QSPI chip select (QMI CS1) for the log flash**: all six QSPI pads are bonded out on RP2354B and GPIO0/8/19/47 can serve as QMI CS1 per Raspberry Pi "Hardware design with RP2350" SS3.1-3.2; QSPI_SS is reserved for BOOTSEL and cannot be reused as a second CS.
- **The QSPI socket becomes an optional back-side expansion (U24, C62, C63 DNP)**: the RP2354B already carries 2 MB of in-package flash and the board already carries a microSD for logs, so a second QSPI device is a *want*, not a requirement — and paying for it in BOM lines, assembly placements and 7.5 x 5.5 mm of front-side area on every board is the wrong default. Marked DNP with the land on B.Cu against the MCU's QSPI pads, it costs nothing but copper and a silk legend, and a user who wants 8 MB of NOR or a PSRAM hand-solders one SOIC-8 and two 0402s. Consequences: the 25 mA flash burst leaves the V3V3_SYS budget; the nets, the pin checks and R35 are unchanged; the front pocket under the MCU goes to the MCU decoupling; and "single-sided assembly" now means "nothing fitted is on the back" rather than "nothing at all is on the back", which `check_back()` enforces by asserting the DNP attribute rather than trusting the placement table.
- **C19 100 uF / 6.3 V polymer (EIA-3528 B) rather than 220 uF / 10 V (EIA-7343 D)**: C19 is the V5_SYS hold-up across the TPS2121's 100 us switchover, so this is a behavioural change, not a package change. Both decks were re-run at 100 uF nominal / 80 uF derated and every predicate still passes with ~0.9 V of margin to the 3.5 V V5_SYS floor (worst case 4.374 V at 0.8 A; see "Open items"), which is what licenses the 43.6 -> 14.0 mm2 of courtyard it gives back. 6.3 V on a 5.0 V rail is the normal 1.26x polymer derating; a tantalum electrolytic would need 2x and would not qualify, so the ESR <= 40 mOhm / polymer wording is load-bearing, not a preference.

- Sensor SPI chip-select lines were floating -> added R48-R51 10k pull-ups to V3V3_ANA.
- ERC's single global label check was disabled -> re-enabled (0/0 with it on).

## Heat and interference

- Enclosed, no forced airflow; qualification ambient 60 C; junctions below 100 C; exposed-pad vias and ground copper are the heat path.
- 4-layer SIG-GND-PWR-SIG: solid L2 ground; L3 pours for VBAT/5V_IN/V5_SYS/V3V3_SYS with ground fill; **U26 and L3 at the bottom-left corner next to the VBAT pad**, its input loop (C73/C74 to VIN/GND) as small as possible and the SW node small and quiet; U7 and its inductor between U26 and the MCU, SW loop over L2 ground, shielded inductor, FB sense after the bead routed away from SW; U12 and the sensors on the ground-referenced analog island near the centre, away from both switchers, the ESC row and any inductor — U12 now sits at the top of that island rather than in the regulator band beside U7/L2, which is both what this line always asked for and what freed the 3.4 mm under U7 that the post-bead output caps C23/C24 needed; no inductor under the IMU/barometer; servo and ESC return currents off the sensor ground; magnetometer external, away from power leads and the ESC. The board sits directly above a 4-in-1 ESC switching motor currents, so the IMU cluster's ground reference and the VBAT/5V_IN loops are the two things that decide whether the sensors are usable.

## Open items and validation

- PCB layout not done: see **Physical design** for the envelope, stack-up, mounting pattern and edge allocation that placement has to satisfy. Final board size is set at placement (the smallest square that fits). The VBAT and 5V_IN copper must be sized for the buck's 2 A plus the servo draw, not just the avionics budget.
- Passive MPNs: L20 AOTA-B201610S3R3-101-T and L3 XGL4030-472MEC assigned; the 22 uF ceramics now name parts (GRM188R61A226ME15 at 10 V 0603, GRM21BR61E226ME44 at 25 V 0805, GRM21BR61H106KE43 for C73). The rest stay open. The buzzer and its driver are gone entirely — a buzzer is now a firmware-free integration choice on one of the four exposed spare IO block rows (11-14, GPIO44-47), not a board feature.
- **The ngspice decks carry nominal capacitance, not derated.** `simulations/*.cir` set `COUT=66u` for the 3.3 V stage, but C10-C12 are now 22 uF / 10 V X5R **0603**, which lose ~40 % at 3.3 V DC bias (~40 uF effective first stage, ~26 uF after the bead). The modelled rail is optimistic by that margin; re-run the decks with the derated value before treating any transient result as a bound. The package change did not change the nominal value, only the bias sensitivity (an 0805 X7S lost ~20-25 %).
- **C19 is now 100 uF nominal / 80 uF derated in the decks** (`CPOLY` in `simulations/integrated_power.cir` and `simulations/source_handover.cir`, and the corner substitutions in `tools/run_power_checks.py`). New V5_SYS minima after the change: integrated nominal 4.4146 V (was 4.4224), lossy_path 4.4129 V (was 4.4204), usb_only 4.7515 V and vbat_only 4.9075 V unchanged; handover worst case (RON 100 mOhm, IRCB 2 A, VRCB 50 mV, CSYS 14 uF, CPOLY 80 uF) 4.403 V at 0.5 A and 4.374 V at 0.8 A (was 4.415 / 4.401). Predicate floor is 3.5 V, so the margin is 0.87-0.90 V. The real part's ESR is the open question, not its capacitance: the model assumes 40 mOhm and a 6.3 V polymer at 5.0 V bias loses far less capacitance than a ceramic, but the MPN is still provisional.
- **Buck output capacitance nominal 66 uF (C76/C77/C80 each 22 uF), derated to ~44 uF per AP63205 EVB guide.** C73 10 uF/50 V at 25 V input bias and all three 22 uF/25 V output caps at 5 V output bias each lose roughly half to DC-bias derating; C73's ~5 uF nominal remains open pending actual footprint measurements.
- **J3 pad pitch is unconfirmed.** MicoAir publishes no pad drawing for the AM32 4-in-1 FC row; 2.00 mm is assumed. The pad *order* is authoritative. Measure the physical ESC before fab.
- **WS2812C VDD.** Its absolute maximum supply is 5.3 V and V5_SYS can be USB VBUS, which is not guaranteed below that on a non-compliant charger (the TPS2121's OV2 is grounded, so the USB channel has no over-voltage cut-off). Acceptable risk or a reason to add OV2 — lead's call.
- **Optional series resistors on the PWM outputs, not yet added** (applies to PWM1-4 on the ESC pads as well as PWM5-8 on the IO block).
- Enclosure thermal measurement needed before sustained loads above 500 mA (see Heat and interference).
- Firmware: ICM-45686 (U21) defaults to I2C/I3C protocol interpretation on its AP interface pins after power-up (DS-000577 Rev 1.0 Sec 10.1); since AP_SCLK/AP_SDA (SENS_SCK/SENS_MOSI) are hardwired as pure SPI signals here, firmware must assert IMU_CS (AP_CS, pin 12) low for the first SPI transaction before relying on SPI0 responses (Table 10 pin 12 note: "Connect to VDDIO if using AP I3C/I2C interface").
- 3D models: every footprint the board uses now resolves inside the project (`MARV_Packages.pretty` + `MARV_Packages.3dshapes`; provenance, licences and SHA256 in `MARV_Packages.3dshapes/PROVENANCE.md`). - MCU sheet: VBUS sense and reset/power-fail supervision (PWR_GOOD is the TPS62913 PG) still to review; servo count / signal voltage on the IO block rows; inductor saturation vs the TPS62913 current limit; ferrite bead DC resistance vs load regulation.
- Hardware: source insert/remove with logging active, max load, no-airflow soak, USB enumeration, rail noise measurements on V3V3_SYS and V3V3_ANA.
- Checks: `kicad-cli sch erc` (0/0, single global label check enabled), netlist export to `reports/power-netlist.xml` + `tools/check_power_netlist.py <netlist>` (223 critical pin mappings, plus the VBAT -> U26 -> 5V_IN -> U25 -> V5_SYS boundary, the ESC pad-row order, the J6/J7/J8 IO-block row map, the ten sensor test points, the 48-GPIO pin plan with its 4 exposed / 6 unexposed spare split, the PWM1-4 / PWM5-8 split, and the DNP set — U24/C62/C63 must carry `dnp` + `exclude_from_bom`, nothing else may, and R35 must not), `tools/run_power_checks.py` (behavioral ngspice; simulations/README.md carries a Status line; non-zero exit on any violated predicate — the decks start at the buck output and prove nothing about U26 itself), PDF in `reports/`, and `tools/audit_footprints.py <netlist>` (every component has a resolvable footprint file and 3D model). 140 components, 105 nets.
