# MARV_Packages.3dshapes — 3D model provenance

Every STEP file in this directory, where it came from, under what terms, and what
is *not* trustworthy about it. Referenced from `MARV_Packages.pretty/*.kicad_mod`
as `${KIPRJMOD}/MARV_Packages.3dshapes/<name>.step`, so the project is
self-contained for layout: no `KICAD10_3DMODEL_DIR` dependency for these parts.

All twelve files were checked to start with `ISO-10303-21;` and to declare
millimetres (`SI_UNIT(.MILLI.,.METRE.)`).

Sizes and SHA256 are of the files as committed.

Caveats:

- **HRO USB-C**: EasyEDA/LCSC-contributed, not the manufacturer's. Dimensions
  should be checked against the HRO drawing
  (`http://www.krhro.com/uploads/soft/180320/1-1P320120243.pdf`, which the
  KiCad footprint itself cites) before trusting it for mechanical fit, enclosure
  cut-outs or board-edge overhang. Its geometry is authored in the EasyEDA 2D
  frame (Y down), i.e. mirrored with respect to KiCad's 3D frame; the part is
  left/right symmetric, so `(rotate (xyz 0 0 180))` reproduces the correct solid
  — but the laser-marking text on the top face reads mirrored. Cosmetic only.
- **Coilcraft**: each STEP is a *series* body; one model covers every
  XGL4030-xxx / XGL3020-xxx inductance value. Both are drawn at the **maximum**
  body size (4.3 x 4.3 and 3.2 x 3.2), not the datasheet nominal 4.0 x 4.0 /
  3.0 x 3.0 that the footprints' F.Fab outlines use. Both are authored Y-up with
  the terminals separated along model X, so both are referenced with
  `rotate -90 0 0`; the XGL4030 also needs `offset 0 0 0.1` because its
  terminations sit at model Y = -0.1, while the XGL3020's bottom is already at
  model Y = 0 and needs no offset (see LIBRARIES.md "Alignment").
- **Molex**: a TraceParts-generated derivative of the Molex CAD, not the raw
  Molex file. The original download archive (`molex_1040310811_STEP_AP242.zip`,
  295289 bytes, SHA256
  `b7b04fd89a11370f3727a29757d904bc10d7f401d1b8dfc49b3523dd0ea18fe4`) and its
  TraceParts metadata sidecar were not committed.

## 2. FreeCAD-generated simplified models

Written by the scripts in `tools/3d/` (see `tools/3d/README.md` for how to
re-run them and for the full dimension tables in each script's docstring).
They exist because no public STEP is downloadable for these packages. They are
dimensionally-correct *simplified* bodies: flat rectangular leads instead of the
real lead-frame profile, a flat exposed pad, a pin-1 corner chamfer plus a top
dot marker, and no material colours.

Caveats that matter for layout:

- **`LED_WS2812B-2020...` lens step is approximate.** The datasheet Side View
  dimensions only the two heights (0.84 total, 0.28 base); the upper moulding's
  width is scaled off the drawing, not read from it. The outer envelope
  (2.20 x 2.00 x 0.84) and the four terminals are dimensioned values.
- **`Texas_VQFN-HR-12...` height is assumed 0.9 mm**: TI drawing 4224010/A gives
  only "1 MAX" for RUX0012A, with no nominal. 0.9 mm was chosen to match the
  0.8/1.0 min/max band of the sibling RPU0010A package.
- **`Texas_RPU0010A...` height 0.9 mm** is the midpoint of the datasheet's
  0.8 min / 1.0 max; no nominal is published either.
- TI offers no direct STEP for either part (Ultra Librarian only, behind a
  T&C-acceptance form plus reCAPTCHA); that is why these are generated rather
  than downloaded.

## 3. KiCad library models reused as dimensional stand-ins

Copied unmodified from the `kicad-packages3d` 10.0.6 package
(`/usr/share/kicad/3dmodels/Package_LGA.3dshapes/`), renamed, and aimed at a
project-local footprint. They are the right body size, pad count and pitch — they
are **not** the manufacturer's models and their land patterns are only
approximately those of the real parts.

All hashes are byte-identical to the installed KiCad files, so the copies are
verifiable against any `kicad-packages3d` 10.0.6 installation.

- **ICM-45686**: TDK's download pages are session-gated and expose no direct
  STEP. Same 2.5 x 3 x ~0.8 mm body, 0.5 mm pitch, 14 pads. Referenced with
  `rotate 0 0 0`; pad geometry and pin-1 corner were verified by the lead against
  DS-000577 Rev 1.0 Sec 11.2 (page 52 top view: pin 1 at the top of the left
  edge, numbering counterclockwise), and the generic model's pin-1 marker agrees
  with that corner.
- **BMP581**: Bosch publishes no STEP for the BMP581. The ST HLGA-10 stand-in has
  the same 2 x 2 mm body and 10 pads, but a different border layout: its
  three-pad rows / two-pad columns are the transpose of the BMP581's. Referenced
  with `rotate (xyz 0 0 90)`, which is the rotation that lands the model's pin-1
  dimple (model XY -0.75, +0.75) on the BMP581's pin-1 corner (footprint +x/-y,
  the chamfered F.Fab corner next to pad 1). Verified by `kicad-cli pcb export
  step --include-pads`: six of the ten model pads then sit exactly on copper, the
  remaining four (the two-pad rows) are 0.25 mm inboard of their pads. Cosmetic
  — it is a stand-in body, not a land-pattern reference.
