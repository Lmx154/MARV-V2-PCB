# MARV V2 design specification

## Purpose and scope

Flight controller for rockets and drones, 5 V-in only (pack-to-5 V conversion lives off-board in a BEC/BMS module — see Decisions). MCU **RP2354B** (QFN-80, 2 MB in-package flash). Onboard sensors: BMI088, BMP581, ADXL375. Logging: QSPI NOR flash and a latched (push-push) microSD socket. External daughterboards on generic ports: GPS (UART), ELRS radio (UART), magnetometer (I2C) — they carry their own circuitry. Design rule: the simplest form of what the user states; for unspecified details, what flight controllers in the wild do.

The schematic implements power, ports, MCU, sensors, logging and PWM outputs: 8 sheets, 129 components, 101 nets, generated by `tools/build_power.py` (idempotent — it overwrites generated sheets, so edit the generator, not the sheets). Firmware and PCB layout are not done. Not for manufacture or flight.

## Power inputs

| Interface | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| J3 5V_IN | XT30PW-M (right-angle, 15 A rated); pin 1 GND, pin 2 +5V_IN. Regulated 4.75-5.5 V only: a BEC, a 1S BMS+boost module, or any 5 V module. A BEC above the mux's OV lockout (~5.86 V typ) is rejected from V5_SYS; raw packs must never be connected. On-net: C16 100 nF, C67/C68 2 x 4.7 uF, C69 100 uF polymer, D23 SMAJ5.0A TVS. |
| J4 USB-C | USB 2.0 data and 5 V bench power; no PD; no on-board current limiter (standard FC practice); startup budget is a firmware matter. |
| V5_SYS | U25 TPS2121 priority power mux: IN1 = 5V_IN (priority), IN2 = USB_VBUS, OUT = V5_SYS. See Decisions and the mux paragraph below for switchover, lockout, current limit and status. |

## Rails and load budget

```
V5_SYS ─┬─ U7 TPS62913 (buck) ─────── V3V3_SYS: RP2354B digital, QSPI
        │                             flash, microSD, MAG port (J9),
        │                             RP2354B VREG_AVDD
        └─ U12 TPS7A2033 (LDO) ────── V3V3_ANA: BMI088, BMP581, ADXL375,
                                       RP2354B ADC_AVDD
```

| Rail | Budget | Notes |

| V3V3_SYS | 500 mA continuous / 800 mA peak <= 10 ms (typical ~300 mA) | TPS62913 rated 3 A |
| V3V3_ANA | <= 50 mA (TPS7A2033 rated 300 mA) | 100 nF at each ADC_AVDD / VREG_AVDD pin; datasheet-rated < 20 uV RMS noise, < 10 uV RMS ripple with its ferrite-bead output filter — verify on hardware |
| GPS/ELRS ports (J6/J7) | 4-pin JST-GH, Pixhawk DS-009 order (1 VCC, 2 TX, 3 RX, 4 GND); VCC = V5_SYS | not in the V3V3_SYS budget |

| Servo / propulsion | external only, raw 5V_IN upstream of U25 | never from V3V3, V5_SYS or USB |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

U7 TPS62913RPUR (VQFN-HR-10 2x2 mm), 2.2 MHz spread-spectrum (S-CONF 6.04k), L2 2.2 uH (XGL4030-222, 7 A), 3x22 uF first-stage output, bead BLE18PS080SN1 (8.5 Ohm, 4 mOhm) + 2x22 uF after it, FB sensed after the bead, divider 15.8k/4.99k (3.33 V nominal, 3.30-3.38 V over VFB tolerance), NR/SS 470 nF, 2.2 nF VIN bypass, PG pulled up to V3V3_SYS. U12 TPS7A2033PDBVR (fixed 3.3 V, active discharge), 1 uF in / 1 uF out, EN driven by PWR_GOOD (the TPS62913 PG signal) so V3V3_ANA rises only after V3V3_SYS is up. Passive MPNs beyond the inductor and bead remain open (see Open items).

Modelled conversion loss (simulations/README.md): buck ~0.09 W at 300 mA, ~0.14 W at 500 mA (92% assumed efficiency), LDO ~0.02 W. Treat 500 mA as a burst ceiling until the real enclosure is measured.

Source mux (U25 TPS2121, IN1 = 5V_IN priority, IN2 = USB_VBUS, reverse-current blocking on both inputs at all times): PR1 divider R42 32.4k/R43 10k selects IN1 above ~4.5 V rising (typ), releases it below ~4.4 V falling, with USB taking over V5_SYS within the ~100 us switchover time (CP2 grounded: internal-reference priority mode). OV1 divider R44 45.3k/R45 10k rejects IN1 above ~5.86 V typ (5.59-6.08 V over tolerance) — this protects V5_SYS from an over-voltage BEC or a mis-plugged 2S pack, but SERVO_5V (J13) is wired upstream of U25 and is NOT protected: a bad source on J3 still reaches the servo row. ILM R46 80.6k sets a ~1.5 A OUT current limit (OCP trips at 2.4x). SS C70 100 nF gives a ~6 ms soft-start ramp. ST (open-drain, HIGH = IN1 selected) drives PWR_SRC_ST -> GPIO39, pulled up by R47 10k to V3V3_SYS. C71/C72 100 nF bypass IN1/IN2.

## Decisions

- **5 V-in only, no on-board pack conversion**: chemistry-agnostic regulated 5 V input (4.75-5.5 V: BEC, 1S BMS+boost, or any 5 V module); no HV buck or its thermal load on the board; servo/ESC current never crosses the board's regulated rails or the mux.
- **U25 TPS2121 priority mux over dual LM66100 ideal diodes**: LM66100's recommended-operating max of 5.5 V and absolute max of 6 V sit too close to real BEC outputs, and it offered no over-voltage lockout, no current limit, and up to 1 A of reverse-activation current; TPS2121 (22 V abs-max) gives all three — OV lockout, ILM current limit, reverse-current blocking — in one part.
- **Split V3V3_SYS (buck) / V3V3_ANA (LDO)**: flash and microSD are supply-transient sources so they stay on the 3 A buck with generous local bulk; the LDO carries only the MEMS sensors and RP2354B ADC_AVDD (~10-20 mA), so its ~20-35 mW dissipation at 1.7 V drop is free and its noise floor beats a generic buck's.
- **BMI088 SDO1 + SDO2 both wired to SPI0 MISO**: reviewed and confirmed correct per datasheet 6.1 (each SDO drives only while its own CSB is low); a reviewer's "tie SDO2 to GND" objection applied the I2C-address rule and was rejected. MARV_Power:BMI088_SPI retypes SDO2 as passive for ERC only.
- **GPIO0 as second QSPI chip select (QMI CS1) for the log flash**: all six QSPI pads are bonded out on RP2354B and GPIO0/8/19/47 can serve as QMI CS1 per Raspberry Pi "Hardware design with RP2350" SS3.1-3.2; QSPI_SS is reserved for BOOTSEL and cannot be reused as a second CS.

Review record (defects found and fixed in the schematic review):
- LM66100 rating (5.5 V rec-op / 6 V abs-max) too close to real BEC outputs -> replaced with TPS2121.
- 5V_IN bulk capacitance below the regulator's datasheet minimum, no TVS -> added C67/C68/C69 and D23 SMAJ5.0A.
- J6/J7 JST-GH pin order didn't match Pixhawk DS-009 -> corrected to 1 VCC, 2 TX, 3 RX, 4 GND.
- VIN_SENSE divider saturated the ADC at 5.5 V input -> widened to R27/R28 22k/10k.
- LDO (U12) had no enable sequencing -> EN now driven by PWR_GOOD, after V3V3_SYS.
- RP2354B VREG_AVDD was split onto V3V3_ANA while VREG_VIN needs to power up with it -> moved to V3V3_SYS.
- Sensor SPI chip-select lines were floating -> added R48-R51 10k pull-ups to V3V3_ANA.
- ERC's single global label check was disabled -> re-enabled (0/0 with it on).

## Heat and interference

- Enclosed, no forced airflow; qualification ambient 60 C; junctions below 100 C; exposed-pad vias and ground copper are the heat path.
- 4-layer SIG-GND-PWR-SIG: solid L2 ground; L3 pours for V5_SYS/V3V3_SYS with ground fill; U7 and its inductor at the J3/J4 edge, SW loop over L2 ground, shielded inductor, FB sense after the bead routed away from SW; U12 and the sensors at the far end on a ground-referenced analog island; no inductor under the IMU/barometer; servo/ESC return currents off the sensor ground; magnetometer external, away from power leads and the ESC.

## Open items and validation

- PCB layout not done: 4-layer stack-up; the 5V_IN -> J13 servo path must be sized for the XT30's rated current, not the board's own load budget.
- Passive MPNs: L20 full P/N AOTA-B201610S3R3-101-T assigned; D22 and BZ1 still need real MPNs.
- Enclosure thermal measurement needed before sustained loads above 500 mA (see Heat and interference).
- Firmware: BMI088 accelerometer starts up in I2C mode and needs a CSB1 rising edge before it responds on SPI0.
- Verify the ADXL375 footprint's pin-1 corner in the 3D viewer before fab (see LIBRARIES.md).
- Optional 100-220 Ohm series resistors on the PWM outputs, not yet added.
- MCU sheet: VBUS sense and reset/power-fail supervision (PWR_GOOD is the TPS62913 PG) still to review; servo count / signal voltage / actuator sheet; inductor saturation vs the TPS62913 current limit; ferrite bead DC resistance vs load regulation.
- Hardware: source insert/remove with logging active, max load, no-airflow soak, USB enumeration, rail noise measurements on V3V3_SYS and V3V3_ANA.
- Checks: `kicad-cli sch erc` (0/0, single global label check enabled), netlist export to `reports/power-netlist.xml` + `tools/check_power_netlist.py <netlist>` (143 pin mappings), `tools/run_power_checks.py` (behavioral ngspice; simulations/README.md carries a Status line; non-zero exit on any violated predicate), PDF in `reports/`. 129 components, 101 nets. The models are architectural: no loop stability, EMI, noise, USB compliance or enclosure temperature is proven by them.
