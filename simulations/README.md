# Power simulations

Results live in [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md) (generated by `python3 tools/run_power_checks.py` with ngspice; carries its own `Status:` line) — this file does not duplicate its tables.

Current design: [DESIGN_SPEC.md](../DESIGN_SPEC.md). Evaluated against the V3V3_SYS budget (500 mA continuous / 800 mA peak <= 10 ms, typical ~300 mA) plus <= 50 mA on V3V3_ANA. Chain: 5V_IN — the output of **U26, the on-board AP63205WU buck** that makes a fixed 5.0 V from the 6-25.2 V pack on the ESC pad row J3 — or USB (J4) into U25 TPS2121, a priority power mux (IN1 = 5V_IN priority, IN2 = USB_VBUS, OUT = V5_SYS), then U7 TPS62913 (buck, V3V3_SYS) and U12 TPS7A20 (LDO, V3V3_ANA).

**The decks start at the buck output.** The pack and the VBAT→5 V conversion are *not* modeled: 5V_IN is represented behaviorally as a 5.0 V source behind 0.10 ohm of effective output impedance with a **2 A hard current limit** (the AP63205's rated continuous output; its high-side peak current limit is 2.5 A min / 2.8 A typ / 3.1 A max, Diodes DS41326 Rev. 2-2). Nothing here proves the buck's loop, its efficiency, its thermal behaviour or its hot-plug survival — those are bench items. The 5V_IN node capacitance is now 44.1 uF (C76+C77 buck COUT + C71), down from the ~110 uF the removed XT30 input network carried, which makes every handover corner strictly harder (board fits 3 x 22 uF nominal; the deck keeps the derated 44 uF as the conservative case). No USB current limiter modeled.

**C19, the V5_SYS hold-up, is 100 uF / 6.3 V polymer (`CPOLY=100u`, 80 uF derated at -20%), not the 220 uF / 10 V it was.** The change was made in the decks first and kept only because every predicate still passed. V5_SYS minima after it, against a 3.5 V predicate floor:

| Corner | V5_SYS min at CPOLY=100u/80u | was at 220u/176u |
| --- | --- | --- |
| integrated `nominal` | 4.4146 V | 4.4224 V |
| integrated `lossy_path` | 4.4129 V | 4.4204 V |
| integrated `usb_only` | 4.7515 V | 4.7515 V |
| integrated `vbat_only` | 4.9075 V | 4.9075 V |
| handover `vin5_removed_usb_present` typical, 0.5 / 0.8 A | 4.415 / 4.403 V | 4.422 / 4.416 V |
| handover `vin5_removed_usb_present` worst-case, 0.5 / 0.8 A | 4.403 / 4.374 V | 4.415 / 4.401 V |
| handover `buck_fault_8v4` worst-case, 0.5 / 0.8 A | 4.833 / 4.728 V | 4.833 / 4.728 V |

The binding number is the worst-case handover at 0.8 A: **4.374 V, 0.874 V above the floor**. The CSYS sensitivity sweep (worst-case, 0.5 A) now reads 20u = 4.403 V, 47u = 4.406 V, 100u = 4.411 V — all still >= 3.5 V. What this does *not* prove is ESR: the model assumes 40 mOhm flat, so the MPN has to be a polymer (Panasonic 6TPE100MAZB, KEMET T520/T530 B case), not a general-purpose tantalum.

## Source model

U25 is modeled as a `muxctl` source-selection subckt (UV / OV1 / PR1 comparators and their hysteresis) plus one `muxpath` subckt per input channel (RON, tSW switchover, always-on reverse-current blocking via IRCB/tRCB). There is no body diode in this model: unlike the LM66100 pair it replaced, the TPS2121 blocks reverse current on both inputs at all times.

## Decks
- `integrated_power.cir` — both sources feeding V5_SYS through U25, the TPS62913 buck and the TPS7A20 LDO together, with a 500->800->500 mA V3V3_SYS load step and a constant 10 mA V3V3_ANA load.
- `source_handover.cir` — standalone U25 handover corner: one source (SURVIVOR) present for the whole run, the other (OTHER) removed or inserted at t=15 ms, using the same muxctl/muxpath model as `integrated_power.cir`.
- `enclosure_thermal.cir` — lumped single-node board thermal model driven by the buck+LDO conversion loss from `integrated_power.cir` plus the load budget's electronics heat figures.
- `rail_load_step.cir`, `source_load_trade.cir` — older, independent decks (a hypothetical 3.3 V rail step, and a dedicated-vs-shared actuator source/load trade) kept for reference; not part of the current model and not driven by `run_power_checks.py`.

## Corners (`corners/`)
`nominal`, `lossy_path`, `usb_only`, `vbat_only`, `loss_300ma` — integrated source/load sweeps. `vin5_removed_usb_present`, `usb_removed_vin5_present`, `usb_insert_vin5_present`, `usb_insert_onto_5vin` (typical/worst-case corners, plus a CSYS sensitivity sweep) — handover timing. `buck_fault_6v5`, `buck_fault_8v4` (typical/worst-case) — U25 OV1 lockout corners: with a fixed 5.00 V +/-1% buck and no 5 V input connector left on the board, a **U26 failure** partially passing VBAT onto its own output is the only way 5V_IN can go over-voltage, so these replace the old mis-set-BEC / 2S-mis-plug readings of the same two runs. `thermal_300mA_*`, `thermal_500mA_*` — enclosure thermal-resistance sweeps.

## How to run

```sh
python3 tools/run_power_checks.py
```

Rewrites `RESULTS.md` and exits non-zero if any design predicate is violated. To run a deck manually: `ngspice -b -o simulations/<name>.log simulations/<name>.cir`.

## What these simulations do and do not prove

These are behavioral models (muxctl/muxpath comparator logic for the TPS2121, assumed efficiency figures for the buck/LDO), not vendor SPICE macromodels. A `Status: PASS` in RESULTS.md means architectural margin only, not hardware sign-off. Remaining hardware validation:

- Vendor-model or bench checks for regulator loops, startup, current-limit behavior and source handover.
- USB enumeration/startup/inrush/suspend and higher-current control, including tolerance extremes.
- Real input transients and reverse-current pulses. **There is no TVS on VBAT** (nothing that clamps under 32 V survives a 6S pack) and the board's VBAT capacitance is ceramic only, so the ESC-side low-ESR electrolytic is load-bearing — verify hot-plug ringing at the U26 VIN pin against its 35 V DC / 40 V-400 ms absolute maximum.
- AP63205 loop, efficiency, hot-plug survival and thermals: not modeled here at all.
- Logging and actuator-disturbance tests, and no-airflow enclosure thermal soak.
- Final MPNs, effective capacitance, inductor saturation/temperature and PCB layout.
